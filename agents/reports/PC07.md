### Proof outline

* `derFullOK_header` destructs the (left-associative) `&&` chain of
  `derFullOK` and converts the three `==` tests into byte equalities with
  `beq_iff_eq`.
* `derEnvelope_of_header` folds those bytes plus the `71 ≤ size ≤ 73` window
  back into `derEnvelope = true` (`simp` with `decide_eq_true`).
* `derFullOK_imp_wireSigOK` combines the two, notes `size ≠ 64` from the
  window, and evaluates `wireSigOK = (size == 64) || derEnvelope`.
* `derFullOK_length_exact` peels the final conjunct, `generalize`s the
  `byteAt b 3` match, `cases` it (the `none` branch contradicts `= true`),
  then does the same for `byteAt b (5 + rLen.toNat)`.  The surviving
  `decide (rLen.toNat + sLen.toNat + 7 = b.size)` yields the equality by
  `decide_eq_true_eq`, and `omega` reorders the sum to `7 + rLen + sLen`.
