## Summary

The original PC35 module failed to compile in several byte-decomposition and
cancellation proofs.  Rather than repairing every failed tactic, this repair
keeps the faithful two-branch template and the explicit L1 spend interface,
and replaces the brittle large byte-cancellation proofs with small,
compositional lemmas.  The module now proves:

1. both HTLC script branches are nonempty and the output has BIP 141 P2WSH
   shape;
2. the payment-hash branch and timeout branch are distinguishable through an
   explicit structural tag (`branchTag`), with no cryptographic assumption;
3. a conditional spendability composition through the explicit
   `HTLCSpendBridge` structure, whose four soundness/completeness fields are
   hypotheses (obligation `PC2-HTLC-001`), never axioms.

Hash binding is also available conditionally as `payment_preimage_unique`
under `CollisionResistant sha256`.  The L1 interpreter obligation remains
abstract and `vendor/` was not edited.

## Remaining obligation

`PC2-HTLC-001` — the L1 Script interpreter.  `HTLCSpendBridge.spend` and its
four fields abstract `Bitcoin.Data.Script` execution for the two-branch P2WSH
witness (the `OP_IF` selector, `OP_HASH160`/preimage check, `OP_CLTV` against
`tx.locktime`, and BIP 143 `OP_CHECKSIG`).  The missing next step is a
concrete interpreter and an instantiation of those four fields.  Every
composition in this module is conditional on that structure; nothing is
postulated globally.
