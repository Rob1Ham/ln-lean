## 4. What remains open and the next smallest step

* **`open_channel` is not represented.**  `Messages.Msg` has no
  `open_channel` constructor, and `Handshake.handshakeStep` starts from
  `init`; the `open_channel` arrival is absorbed into
  `HandshakeState.canSendAccept`.  The mission statement "`accept_channel` is
  reachable only after `open_channel`" is therefore represented by the
  corrected claim `accept_channel_reachable_only_after_init` (both `init`
  messages must have been exchanged).  A faithful `open_channel` payload and
  an explicit environment transition are the missing model work.
* **Full outpoint agreement is not provable in the current model.**
  `finalChannelId` deliberately keeps only `funding_txid`; BOLT 2/LDK XOR the
  output index into the last two bytes.  Consequently, for a fixed txid,
  `outputIndex = 0` and `outputIndex = 1` have the same modelled
  `finalChannelId` but different `FundingOutpoint`s.  The proved result is
  the conditional/corrected `FundingAgreement.parties_agree_on_funding_txid`;
  the hypothesis `readyA.channelId = readyB.channelId` is the two-party link
  that a single-endpoint `HandshakeState` cannot derive.  The output-index
  counterexample is represented by `finalChannelId`'s definition, not by a
  claimed theorem.
* **`channel_ready` confirmation semantics are absent.**  BOLT 2 requires the
  funding outpoint to pay the exact agreed script and (for the non-opener)
  to reach `minimum_depth`.  There is no chain view or `FundingConfirmed`
  predicate in the wave-A imports, so no confirmation theorem is claimed.
* **The wire-type result is finite.**  `handshake_wireTypes_nodup` is a
  `decide` check over the five handshake constants, not a general injectivity
  theorem.  The next step can restate the wave-A
  `Protocol.Laws.wireType_injective` over the handshake subset if importing
  `Protocol.Laws` is permitted in a later wave; the current brief limits the
  imports to `Handshake` and `Messages`.
* **Length bounds are byte-count statements.**  `handshake_lengths_bounded`
  uses `Messages.msgLength`; it is not a decoder/serializer correctness
  proof.  It requires explicit DER-size and `init` feature-size hypotheses
  because `Messages` takes signature lengths from supplied bytes and does not
  bound feature blobs.

Next smallest proof step: implement the big-endian XOR derivation in
`finalChannelId` (copy the txid, XOR `outputIndex`'s high and low bytes into
positions 30 and 31 as in `lightning/src/ln/types.rs:42-48`), prove
`txid.size = 32 → outputIndex < 65536 → finalChannelId_injective` (full
outpoint equality), and then strengthen
`FundingAgreement.parties_agree_on_funding_txid` to equality of the whole
`FundingOutpoint`.  After that, add an `open_channel` payload plus its
environment transition.
