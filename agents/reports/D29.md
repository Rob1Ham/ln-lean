## Remaining gap / next step

The missing model object is a final onion payload / `FinalOnionHopData`
together with its construction from an invoice and its TLV decoding.  A model
module should define that record, its `payment_data` decoder, and a sender
constructor whose secret field is the invoice's `paymentSecret`; then the
acceptance premise can be replaced by a model invariant or by the
constructor's definition, yielding an unconditional protocol-path theorem.
The cryptographic derivation of the payment secret (`construct_payment_secret`)
and the preimage relation remain outside this module.
