## Scope caveats

- This module does **not** prove BIP 143 byte equality for the second-stage
  transaction.  `htlcWireDigest` is the model-level wire-path commitment
  (`0x21 ‖ htlcPreimage ‖ HtlcWire.txBytes` hashed under `commitmentTag`), not
  the vendored `signatureHashBip143Impl`; connecting the two is the
  `HtlcWire`/scriptCode lane (`PR-05`, see `H07`/`H09`).  The binding theorem is
  therefore about *the model's* wire-path message, stated at the same trust
  level as the wave-5 structural `htlcDigest`.
- The `HtlcWireContext` supplies the parent commitment txid, the spent
  commitment output index, and the HTLC `scriptPubKey` as opaque byte strings.
  The module does not synthesize the BOLT 3 offered/received scriptCode; that
  is `H08`.  The derived wire fixes version `2`, `nSequence = 0xffffffff`,
  `nLockTime = 0` and the satoshi-floored value only.
- `HtlcWireDigestCollisionFree` is **not** derived from the model's
  `CollisionResistant M.sha256` (which is injectivity on 32-byte inputs only),
  and no global SHA-256 injectivity is assumed.
  `HtlcWireSerializationBinds` is a separate encoding hypothesis; the module
  proves only a fragment of it (`htlcWireSerializationBinds_pair_of_txid_size_ne`)
  and otherwise keeps it explicit.  `wire_digest_binding_not_view_binding`
  shows both are load-bearing.
- View binding is binding of the **floored** HTLC view.  `amount_msat` is
  carried by `SignedHtlcEntry` but is not in the signed wire preimage;
  `wireEntry_signedData_ignores_msat_fraction` proves two HTLCs differing by one
  millisatoshi share a digest and a signature.  The wave-6
  `htlcView_ignores_msat_fraction` is retained unchanged and re-derived here in
  wire form.
- The signing law is keyed by the public key; there is no secret-key model
  (`PR-01`).  C3 is the model's exact `EufCma` predicate, not a computational
  reduction (`CR-01`).
- `HtlcWire` scripts are model-opaque bytes; no P2WSH/witness-program execution
  or `txCheckerFor` acceptance is claimed (`L1-01`/`L1-02`/`L1-07`).
