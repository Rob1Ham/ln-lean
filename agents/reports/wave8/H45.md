## What was added

1. **Concrete finite data.** Two *distinct* `ChainView` records (`aliceView`,
   `bobView`) with height `100` and the same eight-byte tip hash, differing
   only in `lastReorg`; a lagging height-`99` view; depth `5/6/10/11` reorgs;
   a conserving five-bucket ledger of `1_000_000 msat`; and a three-fact
   observation list (funding at `20`, commitment spend at `60`, HTLC claim at
   `80`).
2. **`SameChain` non-vacuity.** `sameChain_alice_bob` proves the common-tip
   hypothesis for two records that are not equal
   (`views_distinct_but_sameChain`), and `laggingView_not_sameChain` shows the
   hypothesis is not universal.
3. **Common-prefix arithmetic.** `commonPrefixHeights = [0,20,60,80,90]` are all
   in the depth-ten prefix; `rewrittenHeights = [91,95,100]` are all outside;
   the boundary is exact at `90` versus `91`.
4. **`OutOfReachOf` transfer.** `outOfReachOf_of_safeAt_of_allowed` proves the
   reusable implication from the budget form `SafeAt` to the per-reorg form
   `OutOfReachOf`; `channelObservations_outOfReach` instantiates it.
5. **Safety instance and non-vacuity.** `channel_reorg_preserves` instantiates
   `reorg_preserves_channel_safety`; `channel_reorg_facts_eq` and
   `channel_reorg_retained_length` show the reorg filters *nothing* and the
   three facts remain; `channel_reorg_not_vacuous` bundles retention, a
   nonempty fact list, unchanged fact list, and safety at the second distinct
   view.
6. **Sharp boundary.** A fact with exactly `11` confirmations survives depth
   `10` (`boundary_survives_depthTen`) but is evicted by depth `11`
   (`boundary_evicted_depthEleven`); the depth-`11` reorg is not allowed
   (`depthEleven_not_allowed`), so this is a boundary, not a counterexample.
   `outOfReach_not_sufficient_for_budget` mechanizes the gap: one allowed
   reorg can be out of reach while the channel fails `SafeAt` for a
   budget-eleven adversary.
7. **Anti-reorg-delay route.** `channel_reorg_within_antiReorgDelay` instantiates
   `reorg_preserves_channel_safety_within_anti_reorg_delay` with budget `5 < 6`
   on the same two distinct views.
8. **Deep reorgs kept separate.** `deep_reorg_outside_instance` proves every
   reorg at or beyond `antiReorgDelay` is outside the five-block budget;
   `ReorgTargets.deep_reorg_separate_failure_mode` is neither re-proved nor
   weakened.

## Scope caveats

- No Bitcoin consensus and no block-level common-prefix object. `Reorg` still
  carries only `fromHeight`, `depth`, and opaque `newTip` bytes; the replacement
  chain's height is not modelled, so survival is judged at the pre-reorg
  observed tip.
- The concrete heights, hashes, and fact list are finite *model* data, not a
  live-chain vector or a consensus witness.
- `SameChain` is only equality of `height` and `tip`; `lastReorg` is ignored.
  This is demonstrated, not assumed: the instance's two views are distinct.
- `applyReorgAt` filters retention only; it does not recompute confirmations of
  surviving facts and does not rewrite the in-channel ledger. The result is
  retention/eviction, not post-reorg theft or double-spend.
- `antiReorgDelay = 6` is the BOLT 5 / LDK protocol constant, not a consensus
  rule.
- `OutOfReachOf` for a single reorg is strictly weaker than the budget form
  `SafeAt`; `outOfReach_not_sufficient_for_budget` proves the gap on concrete
  data. The main theorem still needs `SafeAt`.
- Deep-reorg behaviour remains the separate
  `ReorgTargets.deep_reorg_separate_failure_mode`; this lane adds no deep-reorg
  preservation claim.
- `NET-01`: no scheduling/fairness/eventual-delivery theorem is added; this lane
  only sharpens the reorg-safety side (`L1-06`).
