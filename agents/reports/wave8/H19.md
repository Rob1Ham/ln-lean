### Length basis (stated in the module docstring)

BOLT 2 §"The `accept_channel` Message" (`refs/bolts/02-peer-protocol.md:913-929`)
mandatory fields: `32` temporary id, `4 × u64 = 32`, `u32 = 4`,
`2 × u16 = 4`, `6 × point = 198`; total
`32 + 32 + 4 + 4 + 198 = 270`.  `fieldWidths` lists each field and
`fieldWidthSum_eq` proves the sum is `270`; `mandatoryPayloadLength_eq_fieldWidthSum`
ties the serialization constant to that list.  This matches the pre-existing
`msgLength_acceptChannel` (`Messages.lean:449-450`) through
`payloadLength_eq_msgLength`, so the additive record cannot drift from the wire
model.  The optional `accept_channel_tlvs` (types 0 and 1, `:931-938`) are
variable length and are deliberately excluded from the fixed sum; they are
modelled additively by `AcceptChannelTLVs`/`tlvBytes`.

## Scope caveats

- **Mandatory fields only.**  The record is the complete *fixed-width*
  `accept_channel` field set (fourteen fields, funding key included).  The
  `accept_channel_tlvs` stream is modelled separately and additively; no TLV
  parsing, `shutdown_scriptpubkey` validity, or `channel_type` feature-bit
  semantics is claimed.
- **Semantics not modelled.**  BOLT 2's `accept_channel` requirements
  (`channel_reserve_satoshis`/`dust_limit_satoshis` inequalities,
  `minimum_depth`, matching `channel_type`, reserve/`max_accepted_htlcs`
  checks) remain in the handshake/conformance layers; this module proves wire
  shape, length, and projection only.
- **Key transport, not key attribution.**  The funding key is proved to survive
  every projection into `FundingNegotiation`/`FundingParams`, but nothing here
  proves the key belongs to the peer.  Attribution still requires the explicit
  transport/registration hypothesis of `PR-01` (H20–H24).
- **`Valid` is exact for the wire model.**  `wellFormed_toAcceptChannel_iff`
  shows the existing `msgLength ≤ 65535`, `sigsValid`, and `semanticOk`
  conjuncts are discharged for every accept payload, so validity reduces to the
  32-byte id and six 33-byte points.  This is a statement about
  `Protocol.Messages`, not about canonical DER/point encoding.
- **No `DecidableEq`.**  `BEq` is structural over `ByteArray`; equality-based
  reasoning about payloads is available, decision procedures are not.
- **No `HandshakeState` change.**  The record is defined in this module; adding
  `acceptChannel?` to `HandshakeState` and setting it in `recvAcceptChannel` is
  lane H20's patch, not this lane's.
