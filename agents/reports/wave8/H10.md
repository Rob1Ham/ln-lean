## Scope caveats

- **Exact domain.** `reverseBytes` is total on `ByteArray`, but the txid
  interpretation is claimed only on `IsTxidBytes b := b.size = 32`. The subtype
  `TxidBytes` is the domain of the display/internal bijection; the theorems
  `reverseBytes_isTxidBytes_iff`, `TxidBytes.displayToInternal_bijective`, and
  `reverseBytes_maps_txidBytes` state this explicitly. No length-32 premise is
  needed for the *field* equality theorems, which are pure projections.
- **No SHA-256d recomputation.** The concrete 32-byte pair is taken from the
  published BOLT 3 Appendices A/C. The module proves the byte-order relation
  between the two forms, not that `SHA256d(funding tx)` equals either constant.
  No collision-resistance (C1) or txid-uniqueness assumption is used.
- **External hex encoding is out of scope.** The display parameter is an
  arbitrary `ByteArray`; no checksum, grouping, `0x` prefix, or endianness of
  the *hex text* is modelled. The statement is about the 32 raw bytes.
- **Wire convention.** The model convention is fixed by BIP 143/BOLT 3:
  `CommitmentWire.fundingTxid`, `HtlcWire.commitmentTxid`, and
  `Bip143Preimage.outpointTxid` are internal order; display order is their
  reversal. The theorems do not claim which order an external RPC chooses.
- **HTLC coupling.** The HTLC bridge is stated directly on
  `Bip143Preimage.ofTx h.toTx 0 ...`, which is definitionally H07's
  `htlcBip143Preimage`. This lane deliberately does not import `H07` (avoiding
  a dependency on another in-flight lane); the parent can add the one-line
  `rfl` bridge when integrating H07.
- **BOLT 3 vector scope.** `bolt3FundingWire` and `bolt3ScriptCode` are
  minimal bridge instances; the outpoint theorem is independent of the other
  wire fields and of the scriptCode. No claim is made that the instance is a
  complete valid commitment transaction.
