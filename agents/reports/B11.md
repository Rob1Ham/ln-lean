## 4. Open items and next smallest proof step

No `sorry` remains in this module. The following are model boundaries, not
hidden assumptions:

1. **Actual vs required fee.** `commitmentFee` is BOLT 3's *required base
   fee*. The actual on-chain fee can be higher after trimmed HTLC outputs
   and a below-dust funder output (`03-transactions.md:558-580`); those
   trimming amounts are not represented in `Commitment` here. Next step:
   define `trimmedValue` in the A3 commitment module and prove
   `actualFee = commitmentFee + trimmedValue` under the BOLT 3 output rules.
2. **Dust/reserve under a fee increase.** The module proves preservation only
   when the fee does not increase, and proves concrete counterexamples for
   increases. The correct general statement is the post-fee check itself
   (`feeUpdateChecksOk_iff`). Next step: instantiate
   `feeUpdateChecksOk` with the funder's concrete `to_local`/`to_remote`
   output from `commitmentPairOfBalances` and show
   `checkedFeeUpdate` is accepted exactly when
   `feeNew ≤ min(amount − dustLimit, localSat − reserve)` holds.
3. **Funding bound.** `fee_le_funding_of_conserves` and
   `commitmentFee_le_funding_of_wellFormed` take conservation as a
   hypothesis. The zero-HTLC case is discharged by
   `zeroHtlcCommitment_conserves` when the fit hypothesis holds. Next step:
   prove the same conservation for `commitmentPairOfBalances` under
   `fee ≤ funder output` (the current definition uses truncating `Nat`
   subtraction, so the hypothesis is necessary).
4. **Policy checks not modelled.** `max_dust_htlc_exposure_msat`,
   `zero_fee_commitments`, channel-type feature negotiation, in-flight HTLC
   races, RBF/package relay fee bumping, and Bitcoin Core's concrete dust
   threshold are outside this module.
