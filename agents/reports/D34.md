## Remaining gap / next step

The model has no field bounding `FeeEnvironment.atHeight`, no link between
`RevokedCommitment.penaltyFee` and the fee environment, and no weight on
`PenaltyTx`.  `penaltyValid` does not require fee sufficiency, so a
`penaltyValid` transaction can pay a fee that the spiked rate will not relay.
The missing invariant is `LN-PENALTY-ECON`: at broadcast time the penalty fee
must cover the model's fee at the spiked rate (`FeeRate.fee`, the ceiling used
by `penaltyNet`; `feeForWeight` is the floored commitment variant), and the
value at stake must exceed that fee.  Next smallest step: add a bounded-feerate field to
`FeeEnvironment` (or a `PenaltyFeeSufficient` predicate relating
`penaltyFee`, `w`, and `env`), then derive the unconditional catalog claim
from `waveD_d34_economical_under_bounded_spike`.
