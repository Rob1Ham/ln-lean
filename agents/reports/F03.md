# F03 — No-underflow and affordability

## Target

- Marker: `LN-WF-F03`
- Module: `LnLean.Proofs.ModelHardening.F03`
- Parent obligation: `LN-CH-UNDERFLOW`
- Imports: `LnLean.Channel.{Commitment,State,Laws}`,
  `LnLean.Proofs.Channel.ReserveProps` (helpers only; no `ModelHardening`
  import).

## Result

- Status: conditional (with mechanized counterexample)
- Build: RC 0 (`lake build LnLean.Proofs.ModelHardening.F03`, 15 jobs)
- New definitions:
  - `NoUnderflow available debit` — the reusable `Prop` that `debit ≤
    available`, i.e. `Nat.sub` does not truncate.
  - `residual available debit` — the exact remainder `available - debit`.
  - `balanceAvailableFor b side` / `debitBalance b side amount` — the
    per-side balance debit used by `update_add_htlc`.
  - `Affordable b side amount` — `NoUnderflow` specialised to a balance.
  - `ReserveAffordable b reserveSat amount` — debit + reserve guard
    (`reserve * 1000 + amount ≤ localBalance`).
  - `LedgerBalanced c fee` — `total + inFlight + fee = fundingValue`.
  - `addLocalHtlc c amount` — arithmetic core of `update_add_htlc` (debit
    local balance, append one `offered` HTLC).
  - `CommitmentFunded funding toLocal fee htlcTotal anchors` — the funding
    covers fee + holder output + HTLC outputs + anchors.
  - `zeroChannel` — concrete zero-funding channel used by the negative
    results.
- Main theorems:
  - `noUnderflow_iff`, `residual_add_eq`, `residual_nonneg`,
    `residual_zero_of_not_noUnderflow`: the invariant is exactly the add-back
    equation; without it the residual saturates at 0.
  - `affordable_iff_residual`, `debit_local_total`, `debit_remote_total`: an
    affordable debit removes exactly its amount from the total balance.
  - `reserveAffordable_imp_affordable`,
    `reserveAffordable_preserves_reserve`, `reserveAffordable_residual`:
    the BOLT 2 reserve guard implies affordability and preserves the reserve.
  - `committedUpdate_preserves_ledger`: **ledger conservation** for a local
    HTLC add under `Affordable`.
  - `step_preserves_affordable`, `step_preserves_ledgerBalanced`: the
    invariant and ledger law are stable under every legal `Step`.
  - `settledCommitment_conservation_of_funded`,
    `settledCommitment_toRemote_eq_residual`,
    `settledCommitment_residual_add`,
    `settledCommitment_toRemote_nonneg`: under `CommitmentFunded`, outputs +
    fee = funding and `to_remote` is the exact non-negative residual.
  - `zeroHtlc_conservation_of_funded`: zero-HTLC special case.
  - `committedUpdate_ledger_counterexample`,
    `committedUpdate_preserves_ledger_not_unconditional`,
    `residual_add_eq_not_unconditional`,
    `zeroHtlc_conservation_not_unconditional`: mechanized refutations of the
    unconditional laws (truncated subtraction).
  - `commitmentFunded_witness`, `zeroChannel_add_zero_ledger`: the new
    predicates are satisfiable and the conditional theorem has a concrete
    instance.
- Added hypotheses: every affirmative ledger/conservation theorem carries
  `Affordable`, `ReserveAffordable`, or `CommitmentFunded`.  No affirmative
  theorem is stated unconditionally.
- Statement fidelity: the unconditional committed-update ledger statement is
  kept verbatim as `committedUpdate_preserves_ledger_unconditional` with the
  single intentional `sorry` and the marker.  It is not claimed true: the
  adjacent counterexample proves its negation.  The corrected statement adds
  only the no-underflow side condition; no other hypothesis is weakened or
  added, and `fee`, funding, and the HTLC list are unchanged by the update.

## Missing model field / next step

`LnLean.Channel.Step` has no `update_add_htlc` constructor and its balance
never changes, so the committed update is a named operation, not a
transition (`LN-CH-101` in `ReserveProps`).  Next step for
`LN-CH-UNDERFLOW`: add BOLT 2 `update_add_htlc` to the relation together with
an `Affordable`/`ReserveAffordable` admission premise, then derive
`committedUpdate_preserves_ledger` from `Step` rather than from the operation.
Such a constructor would discharge the one `sorry` and reduce the parent
obligation to a proved transition law.  A second gap is that `Step` carries
no `update_fulfill_htlc` / `update_fail_htlc`, so the return path of the
in-flight bucket is still outside the relation.
