## Why the removed statement was false

`SigVerifier` is an arbitrary `Bool`-valued function.  An always-accepting
verifier can accept an empty signature, so acceptance does **not** imply a
64-byte signature.  The counterexample is retained and proved:

- `waveE_e40_empty_signature_accepted_counterexample`
- `waveE_e40_acceptAll_emptySigUpdate_changes`
- `waveE_e40_acceptAll_not_lengthRespecting`

## Strongest retained replacements

- `waveE_e40_accepted_update_signature_length_of_length_respecting`
  — acceptance implies `GossipSignature.Valid` under the explicit admission
  contract `LengthRespecting verify`.
- `waveE_e40_accepted_update_signature_length_lengthChecked`
  — the same result for the wrapper `lengthChecked verify`.
- `waveE_e40_accepted_update_signature_length_iff_lengthRespecting`
  — the exact characterization: the unconditional statement holds **iff** the
  verifier respects signature length.
- `waveE_e40_lengthChecked_rejects_emptySigUpdate`
  — the checked verifier rejects the empty-signature counterexample.
