# D05 — Payment hashes are SHA-256 outputs, relying on assumption C1 (collision and preimage res

- Parent/catalog: `LN-CR-001`
- Module: `LnLean.Proofs.WaveD.D05`
- File: `LnLean/Proofs/WaveD/D05.lean`

## Result

- Status: counterexample
- Build: RC 0 — `flock -w 900 /tmp/ln-build.lock lake build LnLean.Proofs.WaveD.D05`
- Theorems/definitions:
  - `waveD_d05_paymentHashOf_eq_sha256` — `(M.paymentHashOf pre).bytes = M.sha256 pre.bytes` (definitional, after `sha256_size`).
  - `waveD_d05_paymentHashOf_is_sha256_output` — every constructed payment hash has an explicit SHA-256 range witness.
  - `waveD_d05_opensTo_is_sha256_output` — any hash lock satisfied by a preimage is a SHA-256 output.
  - `waveD_d05_opensTo_iff_sha256_output` — `(∃ pre, opensTo M ph pre) ↔ ph ∈ range M.sha256`.
  - `waveD_d05_no_preimage_of_not_sha256_output` — a hash outside the range cannot be opened by any preimage.
  - `waveD_d05_sha256_output_valid`, `waveD_d05_paymentHashOf_valid` — SHA-256 outputs / canonical hashes are 32 bytes.
  - `waveD_d05_opensTo_unique_of_collisionResistant` — preimage uniqueness under C1 (`CollisionResistant M.sha256`, 32-byte form).
  - `waveD_d05_paymentHashOf_commits` — equal canonical hashes of valid preimages imply equal preimages.
  - `waveD_d05_paymentHashOf_ne` — distinct valid preimages yield distinct canonical hashes (contrapositive).
  - `waveD_d05_fulfil_preimage_unique` — two `update_fulfill_htlc` preimages opening one hash are equal under C1.
  - `waveD_d05_updateAddHtlc_hash_is_sha256_output` — an `update_add_htlc` built from `paymentHashOf pre` carries a SHA-256 output.
  - `waveD_d05_updateAddHtlc_no_fulfil_of_not_output` — a non-output `payment_hash` can never be fulfilled.
  - `zeroBytes`, `badPaymentHash`, `badUpdateAddHtlc`, `waveD_d05_badPaymentHash_not_sha256_output`, `waveD_d05_badPaymentHash_not_valid`, `waveD_d05_badUpdateAddHtlc_wellFormed`.
  - `waveD_d05_wellFormed_message_with_non_output_hash` — for every model there is a `wellFormed` `update_add_htlc` whose `payment_hash` is 33 bytes and outside the SHA-256 range.
  - `waveD_d05_not_all_paymentHashes_are_sha256_outputs` — `¬ ∀ ph, ∃ b, M.sha256 b = ph.bytes`.
- Added hypotheses:
  - Binding/uniqueness theorems take `hc : CollisionResistant M.sha256`.
  - Protocol-level "every accepted `payment_hash` is an output" needs the explicit range hypothesis `∃ b, M.sha256 b = ph.bytes` (or construction via `paymentHashOf`); it is not derivable from C1.
- Statement fidelity:
  - The catalog wording mixes two different facts. "Payment hashes are SHA-256 outputs" is **structural** for hashes constructed by `paymentHashOf` or opened by a preimage, and is proved unconditionally; C1 is not needed.
  - "Relying on C1" is correct only for the *binding* consequence (a hash determines at most one preimage), which is proved conditional on `CollisionResistant`.
  - The global `PreimageResistant` field of `PrimitiveModel` was deliberately not used: `LnLean.Proofs.Crypto.Sha256Props.no_injective_hash_into_32_bytes` proves it is inconsistent with `sha256_size`.
  - The literal universal claim over `PaymentHash` is false in the model: `PaymentHash` wraps an unconstrained `ByteArray` and `LnLean.Protocol.wellFormed` does not check the payment-hash width or membership in `M.sha256`'s range. The counterexample is a BOLT 2 type-128 message with a 33-byte `payment_hash` that still satisfies `wellFormed`.

## Remaining gap / next step

The model needs an invariant (not just an assumption) that every accepted
`payment_hash` is in the range of `M.sha256`. Concretely:

1. `LnLean.Protocol.wellFormed` (or a new `paymentHashesValid` conjunct) should
   require `m.paymentHash.Valid` at minimum, and ideally
   `∃ b, M.sha256 b = m.paymentHash.bytes` at the ingress boundary.
2. Failing a range check, the model cannot distinguish an honestly generated
   hash from an arbitrary 32-byte or malformed value, so no C1-based argument
   can establish HTLC solvability for received messages.
3. A future obligation should state the invariant on the receiving side:
   "if the sender's `update_add_htlc` was produced by the protocol, its
   `payment_hash` is a `paymentHashOf` image", then compose with
   `waveD_d05_paymentHashOf_commits` for uniqueness.

The catalog Lean target `LnLean.Crypto.Hash.sha256` is stale: `sha256` is a
field of `LnLean.Crypto.Model.PrimitiveModel`, not a declaration in
`LnLean.Crypto.Hash`. The theorems here target the actual definitions
(`PrimitiveModel.paymentHashOf`, `opensTo`, `Protocol.UpdateAddHtlc`).
