## 3. Proof approach

`htlcSet` and `removeFromSet` are both `List.filter` (on `isLive` and on
`decide (x.id ≠ id)` respectively), so LN-HTLCRecord-010 is a filter-filter
commutation:

* `unfold htlcSet removeFromSet` exposes the two nested filters;
* `rw [List.filter_filter, List.filter_filter]` pushes both to a single filter
  over `hs`, producing predicates `fun a => isLive a && decide (a.id ≠ id)`
  and `fun a => decide (a.id ≠ id) && isLive a`;
* `List.filter_congr` reduces the goal to pointwise `Bool.and_comm`.

`totalHtlcValue` is `(hs.map (·.amountMsat.val)).sum`, so
LN-HTLCRecord-011 is an induction on the list:

* `[]`: `0 ≤ 0`.
* `h :: t`: the pointwise bound gives `h.amountMsat.val ≤ m`, and the
  induction hypothesis applied to the tail gives
  `totalHtlcValue t ≤ t.length * m`.  After
  `List.length_cons`, `Nat.add_mul`, `Nat.one_mul`, the goal is rewritten with
  `change` to `h.amountMsat.val + totalHtlcValue t ≤ t.length * m + m`, which
  `omega` closes.

No helper lemmas or definitions were needed or added.
