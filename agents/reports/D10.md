## Remaining gap / next step

The claim is proved unconditionally for the wave-A abstract frame.  Two
model boundaries remain, both outside framing:

1. The two-byte transport length header is modelled in plaintext; the real
   header is inside the Noise-encrypted transport
   (`Crypto.Noise` / `peer_channel_encryptor.rs`).  A future module could
   connect `waveD_d10_decode_some_iff` to the decrypt-then-check-length path.
2. The decoder checks only framing length, not the per-message-type body
   length (`msgs.rs` readers).  BOLT 1's "insufficient length for the known
   encoding" rule is a separate message-body obligation.

No `sorry`/gap remains in `D10.lean` itself.
