### Statement fidelity

**Target 27 / `LN-RT-006`.**
The catalog claim is: "The gossip graph contains only channels with valid announcement signatures." BOLT 7 requires all four `channel_announcement` signatures (`nodeSignature1`, `nodeSignature2`, `bitcoinSignature1`, `bitcoinSignature2`) to verify before admission.

- D27 defines `announcementValid verify a := a.signaturesValid verify`, proves the checked-store invariant `AnnouncementsValid` under `applyGossipChecked` (`waveD_d27_applyGossipChecked_preserves`), materialises `announcementGraph` only through `bindChannel` (`waveD_d27_announcementGraph_only_valid`), and proves the graph-level result `waveD_d27_gossip_graph_only_valid_channels`: every edge of `(ns.applyMessage verify m).graph` has a stored announcement in `ns.gossip.announcements` whose four signatures verify and whose `short_channel_id` matches. It also proves `waveD_d27_graph_edges_only_valid` with matching endpoints. The raw wave-A path is explicitly refuted by `waveD_d27_raw_applyGossip_admits_invalid` and `waveD_d27_network_invariant_not_free`.
- D27 additionally supplies the catalog spelling as a real alias: `LnLean.Routing.Gossip.announcement_valid` in the `LnLean.Routing.Gossip` namespace, transparently equal to `announcementValid`.
- E27 defines a local `announcementValid`/`announcement_valid` and proves the store-level `storeAnnouncementsValid` invariant under `applyGossipChecked` (`waveE_e27_storeAnnouncementsValid_applyGossipChecked`), plus the binding seam `waveE_e27_binding_edge_valid`. Its graph-level positive claim, `waveE_e27_gossip_graph_announcements_valid`, is exactly the unconditional catalog statement and is left as `sorry`; the module itself proves that the unconditional raw-store reading is false (`waveE_e27_raw_applyGossip_accepts_invalid`, `waveE_e27_raw_applyGossip_breaks_invariant`) and that a `Graph` can contain an edge with no valid announcement (`waveE_e27_graph_edge_without_announcement`).
- Conclusion on fidelity: D27 states the stronger, graph-level conditional form and does not leave a false unconditional theorem with `sorry`; E27 is honest about the model gap but stops at the store level and carries the real `sorry`. D27 is closer to the catalog sentence.

**Target 28 / `LN-PM-002`.**
The catalog claim is: "A BOLT 11 invoice signature is valid for the payee node key." The model's `Invoice.verify` is `i.Valid ∧ sigCheck i.payee (digest i) i.signature = true`, exactly LDK's `Some(pk)` BOLT 11 `check_signature` branch.

- D28 defines `signatureValidForPayee` and proves `waveD_d28_verify_iff` (`Invoice.verify ↔ i.Valid ∧ signatureValidForPayee`), then the main theorem `waveD_d28_signature_valid_for_payee`. It adds `SignatureKeyBinding` for payee uniqueness (`waveD_d28_accepting_key_eq_payee`, `waveD_d28_signing_key_unique`, `waveD_d28_recovered_key_unique`), and `EufCma` plus injective `digest` for `waveD_d28_signature_binds_digest` and `waveD_d28_signature_binds_invoice`. Its negative result `waveD_d28_unconditional_binding_false` constructs two structurally valid invoices with distinct payees, the same digest and the same signature, both accepted by a key-ignoring checker; `waveD_d28_key_binding_not_free` shows `SignatureKeyBinding` is not automatic.
- E28 defines `signature_valid` (the closest spelling to the catalog target) and proves `waveE_e28_verify_implies_signature_valid`, `waveE_e28_signature_valid_iff_verify`, and a congruence lemma. It adds `MessageBinding` and `KeyBinding`, proves `waveE_e28_payee_unique`, and instantiates the checker with `PrimitiveModel.ecdsaVerify` via `ecdsaChecker`; `waveE_e28_ecdsaChecker_messageBinding` derives message binding from `ecdsa_eufCma`, and `waveE_e28_verify_implies_ecdsa_valid` states the concrete ECDSA form. Its negative result `waveE_e28_valid_not_signature_valid` only shows that structural validity does not imply signature validity under an always-rejecting checker.
- Conclusion on fidelity: both prove the model-level claim. D28 has the more directly relevant negative result (same digest/signature cannot be valid for two payees without key binding) and the stronger EufCma/invoice-binding corollaries. E28 has the exact predicate spelling and the useful concrete `PrimitiveModel` bridge, but its `ecdsaChecker` passes the full 65-byte `InvoiceSignature.bytes` to `ecdsaVerify` as an `EcdsaSig`; `EcdsaSig` has no length invariant, so this is a modeling/fidelity caveat rather than a proof error.

### Added hypotheses / model gaps

**D27 / target 27.**
- The graph-level theorem is conditional on `NetworkChannelsValid verify ns`: every `ChannelView` in `ns.views` is backed by a stored announcement in `ns.gossip.announcements` with `signaturesValid`, matching `scid`, `node1`, `node2`, and `capacitySat`.
- The model does not construct `views` from accepted `channel_announcement` messages: `GossipNetwork.applyMessage` records the announcement in `gossip` but leaves `views` unchanged. D27 documents this as the missing model field and proves preservation of the invariant, not its initial existence.
- The raw `GossipState.applyGossip` path has no signature gate; D27's counterexamples make this explicit.
- No cryptographic unforgeability is claimed: `SigVerifier` remains abstract. The report's "only standard axioms" claim was not independently checked with `#print axioms` because this audit did not run Lean.

**E27 / target 27.**
- The positive theorem is conditional on `storeAnnouncementsValid verify s`, and uses the checked path `GossipProps.applyGossipChecked`, not raw `applyGossip`.
- The graph-level claim is not proved; the `sorry` at line 341 is the documented open obligation `LN-WE-E27`.
- The missing link from an admitted announcement to a materialised `ChannelView`/`ChannelEdge` is stated in E27's report but not mechanised at graph level.

**D28 / target 28.**
- `SignatureKeyBinding sigCheck` is the added hypothesis for payee-key uniqueness. It is not a field of `PrimitiveModel` and is not derivable from `EufCma`.
- `EufCma sigCheck` is the existing C3 model assumption; `Function.Injective digest` is an extra premise for binding the whole invoice, and the BOLT 11 HRP/data-part digest construction and bech32 encoding remain unmodelled.
- `sigCheck` and `digest` are parameters; the model does not supply recoverable-ECDSA key recovery or the `n`-field-absent branch.

**E28 / target 28.**
- `KeyBinding sigCheck` is the added uniqueness hypothesis; it is not proved for the concrete `ecdsaChecker`.
- `MessageBinding` is proved for `ecdsaChecker M` from `PrimitiveModel.ecdsa_eufCma`, which is a genuine advantage of E28.
- The concrete checker hands the whole 65-byte BOLT 11 compact signature (including recovery id) to `ecdsaVerify` as its opaque `EcdsaSig`; the model has no 64/65-byte split or recovery-id handling. This should be documented if E28 is ever used as the fallback.
- No recoverable-ECDSA key recovery and no BOLT 11 signable-digest encoding.

## Reasoning

**Target 27: D27 over E27.**
D27 and E27 agree on the central negative fact: raw `applyGossip` has no signature check, so the unconditional catalog statement is false in the wave-A model. Both prove the checked-store preservation lemma. The deciding difference is that D27 proves a graph-level conditional theorem (`waveD_d27_gossip_graph_only_valid_channels`) and a materialisation theorem (`waveD_d27_announcementGraph_only_valid`), while E27's only positive graph-level theorem is a `sorry` (its graph-level negative counterexample is proved). D27 also provides the catalog alias `LnLean.Routing.Gossip.announcement_valid`. D27's added `NetworkChannelsValid` is stronger than E27's store invariant, but it is exactly the missing view-to-announcement link, it is stated in the module docstring and report, and it is not presented as an unconditional theorem. D27's counterexamples are mechanised and at least as strong as E27's. The absence of a `sorry` and the graph-level coverage make D27 the better whole-file version. E27's explicit `sorry` is honest but disqualifying for a final target-27 file unless the integrator intentionally keeps a documented open obligation; I do not recommend that when D27 builds cleanly.

**Target 28: D28 over E28.**
Both files are `sorry`-free, build in their worktrees, and prove the same model-level implication from `Invoice.verify` to the payee-key signature check. The main differences are the conditional theory and the negative result. D28's `waveD_d28_unconditional_binding_false` is the adversarial result that justifies `SignatureKeyBinding`: two verified invoices with distinct payees, identical digest, and identical signature under a key-ignoring checker. In contrast, E28's negative result only shows a structurally valid record can fail an always-rejecting checker. D28 also proves the EufCma digest-binding and invoice-binding corollaries; E28 proves a concrete ECDSA instantiation but does not prove `KeyBinding` for it and has the 65-byte payload caveat. E28's exact `signature_valid` spelling is a real advantage for traceability, but it does not outweigh D28's stronger statement-fidelity/negative-result package for this target. D28 is the safer whole-file choice; E28 is a reasonable fallback only if the integrated tree specifically requires the concrete `PrimitiveModel` bridge, and then its ECDSA payload representation should be reviewed.

**F candidates.**
There is no F27/F28. `F06` is positive-capacity admission and `F07` is 64-byte gossip-signature admission; both address adjacent gossip model gaps and both contain their own target-specific `sorry` obligations. They neither replace D27/E27 (announcement signature validity) nor D28/E28 (invoice payee signature validity). The Wave-F model trees are identical to the main model, so no F model hardening closes these claims.
