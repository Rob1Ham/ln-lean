## Remaining gap / next step

- `commitmentPairOfBalances` emits the two 330-sat anchor outputs when
  `option_anchors` is enabled but does not debit them from the funder; this is
  the Wave-A builder gap also recorded in `LnLean.Proofs.Conservation`.
  To state the pair-level bound unconditionally, the model needs either an
  anchor debit (`2 * anchorOutputSat` subtracted from the funder's output) or
  an explicit invariant forbidding anchors in that constructor.
- The HTLC dust rule in `LnLean.Channel.HTLC` uses
  `htlcOutputValue = floor(amount_msat / 1000) - secondStageFee` and compares
  against `dust_limit_satoshis`; `CommitmentHTLCOutput` already stores the
  post-trim satoshi amount.  Bridging those two representations (and thereby
  proving that the commitment's HTLC list is exactly the non-dust set) is
  left to the HTLC workstream; this module proves the bound for any list of
  already-surviving HTLC outputs and for the filter-based trim abstraction.
