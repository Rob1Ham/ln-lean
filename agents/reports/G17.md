## Reasoning

For target 33, the two generic induction theorems are essentially equivalent: both say that an invariant preserved by every step under every adversary yields `Secure`, and both correctly avoid a fairness hypothesis. The decision is made by the concrete model, and E33 is clearly stronger there. E33 represents an actual asynchronous channel queue, checks the adversary's delivery bit against the destination and the partition relation, and proves that arbitrary operation orders preserve conservation. It also proves that the chaotic schedule delivers every queued message and moves 100 msat in one step, so its safety result is demonstrably non-vacuous. D33's concrete step checks only `delivery n 0`, ignores `partition`, and uses a fixed modulo op stream; its "without fairness" corollary is only an existence statement. D33's `honest := [0]` alongside a `corrupted := [0]` adversary is also a catalog-level inconsistency of the kind `ThreatModelProps` labels `LN-SEC-003`. D33's extra bad-initial-state boundary theorem is useful and can be copied as a supporting lemma, but it is not enough to keep the weaker whole file. Keep E33.

For target 34, the first-order question is whether the counterexample actually establishes the economic claim. E34 does: `atStakeSat` is the sum of the two old outputs, `penaltyNetSat` subtracts the required fee from that gross amount, and at the witness spike the net recovery is negative (and the required fee even makes the sweep negative, outside `MoneyRange`). D34 does not: `d34AtStake` is the penalty transaction's post-fee sweep (1500 sat), not the gross 2000 sat at stake, so `penaltyNet` subtracts the 500 sat penalty fee twice. At D34's own spike, the gross net recovery is `2000 - 1500.001 = 499.999 sat`, i.e. still positive; the theorem's `¬ penaltyEconomical` is true only relative to the already-reduced 1500 sat figure. D34's `¬ feeSufficient` result is valid and important (a valid penalty can underpay the spike fee), but it is not the same claim as "uneconomical".

On the other hand, D34 has the better vocabulary and the correct BOLT 5 weight: it reuses the canonical `penaltyNet` from `ThreatModelProps`, its `FeeScheduleBounded` covers `base` and every `atHeight`, and its `bolt5PenaltyWeightTwoOutputs` is 810, matching BOLT 5's 324 + 272 + 214. E34 duplicates `penaltyNet` in satoshis and, worse, labels BOLT 3's 724 + 172n commitment weight as BOLT 5's penalty weight. That error is conservative for the negative result (at 810 the required fee is 81,000 sat, still far above the 2,000 sat at stake), but it is a factual catalog/BOLT error and it forces the concrete `penaltyFee` / `feePaidPenalty` witness to be updated. E34's catalog-shaped positive theorem also has an unused spike hypothesis; its all-height fee-cap and spike-max theorems are the real positive content.

Because neither whole file is both economically correct and BOLT-correct as written, the honest target-34 decision is a replacement. The replacement is small: keep D34's `penaltyEconomical`, `FeeScheduleBounded`, `effectiveRate`, `waveD_d34_economical_iff_rate_bound`, and `waveD_d34_economical_under_bounded_spike`; take E34's `atStakeSat`/`penaltyNetSat` gross-at-stake comparison, `PenaltyCovered`, `FeeAtMost`/`IsWorstFeerate`, and `MoneyRange` witness; and set the weight to BOLT 5's 810 for the two-main-output case (324 + 272 + 214; 407/413 per HTLC). If the integrator refuses a new file, keep E34 after correcting `penaltyWeight` to 810 and `spikeCommitment.penaltyFee` to 81000, and do not keep D34's section 4. If the integrator instead keeps D34, `d34AtStake` must become the gross `2_000_000 msat` and the spike must be raised to at least `2_000_001 msat/kw` at weight 1000 (or the weight/spike pair adjusted) before the concrete theorem can be cited for the catalog claim.

The F lane does not resolve either target: F01–F16 are model-hardening modules and none defines a fee environment, penalty economics, or an asynchronous safe-transition replacement. There are no Alt files for 33/34. The catalog-target names are still `statementOnly` and must be repointed by the integrator regardless of the selected file.
