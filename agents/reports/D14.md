## Remaining gap / next step

The literal claim about the current `State.Step` is false/underspecified:

1. `Step` changes only phase, commitment counters, and the feerate.  It never
   changes `balance` or `htlcs`, and there is no `update_add_htlc`
   constructor.  Hence no `Step` can enforce (or repair) either bound.  The
   two counterexamples exhibit a legal `commitment_signed` from a normal
   channel with zero balances and 1000-sat reserves, and from a channel with
   one dust HTLC while `max_dust_htlc_exposure_msat = 0`; `step_exposure` and
   `step_reserve_iff` show the violations persist.
2. Missing model fields/invariants: no `max_dust_htlc_exposure_msat` on
   `ChannelParams`; no reserve/dust/exposure guard in `Step`; no
   `update_add_htlc` transition for `Channel.htlcs` (the existing
   `HTLC.addHTLC` acts on the separate `HTLCRecord`); and no link from
   `feerate` to the HTLC-timeout/success fees.  `waveD_d14_exposure_mono_fees`
   proves the monotonicity needed once such a link exists (BOLT 2 warns that
   a higher feerate can trim multiple HTLCs).
3. Next step: add an `update_add_htlc` constructor and these policy fields to
   the model, derive the second-stage fees from `feerate_per_kw` using the
   BOLT 3 663/703 weights, and connect `HTLC.addHTLC`'s success to
   `AdmissibleAdd`.
