### Proof notes

* `htlcSet_removeFromSet_comm`: after unfolding both `htlcSet` and
  `removeFromSet`, both sides are double filters. `List.filter_filter`
  rewrites each to a single filter over `hs`, one with predicate
  `fun x => HTLCRecord.isLive x && decide (x.id ≠ id)` and the other with the
  two conjuncts swapped. `funext` plus `Bool.and_comm` closes the predicate
  equality, so the two filters agree.
* `totalHtlcValue_le_length_mul`: induction on `hs`. The cons case splits the
  sum with `List.sum_cons`, applies the pointwise bound to the head and the
  induction hypothesis to the tail, then uses
  `(t.length + 1) * m = t.length * m + m` via
  `Nat.add_mul`/`Nat.one_mul`/`Nat.add_comm`. No `omega` and no Mathlib.
