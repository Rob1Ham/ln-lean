## Remaining gap / next step

The missing field/invariant is the hash linkage
`FundingParams.witnessScriptHash = sha256 p.witnessScript.bytes` (together with
a concrete `sha256` and its collision-resistance proof, or a crypto-seam axiom
discharged in `LnLean.Crypto.*`). A follow-up should either make this an
invariant of channel construction (`make_funding_redeemscript` followed by
`sha256`, matching rust-lightning `chan_utils.rs:847`) or expose a
`FundingParams` constructor that computes the hash. Also not modelled: L1
script execution, P2WSH spendability, and actual SHA-256. Those remain outside
this module and are not asserted.
