## Reasoning

**Target 39 — D wins on traceability and on modelling "accepted".**
The decisive facts are mechanical, not aesthetic:
1. `D39` is the only 39 candidate whose obligation marker is both adjacent to
   the target declaration and collectable (`D39.lean:69-73`).
2. `D39` is the only 39 candidate that models admission at the *store* level
   (`waveD_d39_applyGossipPositive`, `302-309`) and proves the invariant is
   preserved for all three message kinds (`335-353`). The catalog claim is
   about an *accepted* announcement, so admission belongs at the store, not
   only at `bindChannel`.
3. `D39` keeps the refuted claim visible next to a mechanised counterexample,
   matching the established project pattern in `GossipProps`.

`E39` has zero `sorry`s and the elegant exact equivalence
(`bindChannel_capacity_pos_iff`) plus a clean composition of `bindChannel`.
But it pays for that by deleting the target statement: the only positive
statement is for a new function, and the marker is invisible to the collector.
Zero `sorry` obtained by not stating the obligation is not a fidelity win. If
the parent adopts a strict no-`sorry` release gate, `E39` is the correct
fallback, but only after (a) adding the explicit positive theorem and (b)
attaching a collectable marker.

`F06` is technically close to `D39` and has better graph-level propagation,
but (i) it lives in the wave-F model-hardening lane, not the D/E target pair,
(ii) its marker is inside the proof body and uncollectable, and (iii) its
`applyMessage` propagation is partly vacuous for announcements. `D39` is the
better whole-file choice for this target.

**Target 40 — E wins on fidelity and on the exact-gap theorem.**
`D40` is `sorry`-free, but it deletes the target statement entirely and its
marker is uncollectable; what remains is a set of conditional results plus a
counterexample. `E40` keeps the exact claim (marked, collectable), proves the
contract is *equivalent* to the claim, supplies the same constructive
`lengthChecked` guard, covers update/announcement/node, and mechanises the
counterexample against the real store. `F07` is broader (all message types,
two counterexamples, contract non-universality) but is outside the target
numbering, has no constructive admission wrapper, lacks the necessity
direction, and would likewise need its marker to be re-homed if used for 40.
The `E40` report's own claims match the module (marker, one `sorry`,
conditional trio, `iff`), with one consistency blemish: the E39/E40 report
timestamps show `E39.md` predates its final source, but `E40.md` postdates its
build.

**Cross-cutting facts that affect all choices.**
- All six target files build against the same base (md5-identical
  `Gossip.lean`, `Laws.lean`, `GossipProps.lean`), so no candidate is
  favoured by a changed model.
- The stored artifacts and declaration layouts are consistent for all six,
  but no build was run here; `D40`'s 3-second artifact/source inversion is the
  only hard build-evidence anomaly and it disappears from the chosen set.
- The intended `sorry`s in `D39` and `E40` are on statements *proved false*;
  neither module uses them downstream. No accidental `sorry` was found.
