```lean

  acceptChannelUpdate verify s u ≠ s ∧ ¬ UpdateKeyAnnounced s u
```

`admitChannelUpdate_change_key_announced_after` (proved, `sorry`-free):

```lean

admitChannelUpdate verify s u ≠ s →
```

`admitChannelUpdate_rejects_unannounced`. The key-attribution hypothesis is
