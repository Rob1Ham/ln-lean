### Proof shape

`all_failed_outcomes_not_succeeded` is the trace invariant: if a keysend
session is not `succeeded` and every outcome in the list is `.failed`, then
the resulting session state is still not `succeeded`.  Induction over the
trace; `PaymentSession.settle` maps `pending` to `failed` and is absorbing on
`failed`, so no success can be introduced.  The target then follows by
`unfold revealedPreimage` plus the invariant: `revealedPreimage` is `none`
unless the state is `succeeded`.
