## 4. What remains open, and the next smallest steps

There are **no open proof obligations in this file**. The remaining gaps are
model boundaries, not unfinished proofs:

1. **Route blinding cryptography is not modelled.** `BlindedHop.exposed`
   proves only that the *wire view type* omits `realNode`, `shortChannelId`,
   and the plaintext terms; it does not prove computational unlinkability.
   The next smallest step is a `BlindedCrypto` interface with `rho_i` and
   `openTerms : ByteArray → ByteArray → Option BlindedTerms`, plus an AEAD
   binding proposition (assumption C7) and an ECDH injectivity proposition
   (C5/C8) as explicit hypotheses.
2. **Fee fields are not tied to the amount.** Wave A's `Route` lets the
   caller choose `feeMsat` independently of `amountMsat` and of the amount
   passed to `Hop.ofEdge`. `routeValid` therefore does not check BOLT 4's
   backwards fee computation. The next step is a predicate
   `FeeConsistent r` requiring `feeMsat i = Hop.ofEdge e (amountAt (i+1))`
   and a proof that the backward recursion terminates at the sender amount.
3. **Rounding gap.** `Hop.ofEdge` rounds the proportional term **up**, while
   BOLT 4's inverse formula rounds down. `ofEdge_rounding_counterexample`
   gives the checked instance `base=0`, `ppm=1000`, `incoming=1000`, where
   the forwarding fee exceeds the incoming amount by 1 msat. A production
   router must use the same rounding on both sides; that arithmetic is not
   in wave A.
4. **Distinct channel ids are not implied by `routeValid`.** The reverse
   direction of a BOLT 7 channel has the same `short_channel_id`, so
   `routeValid` accepts a route that traverses the same channel twice.
   `Route.WellFormed` is the separate invariant; the next step is a route
   selector that enforces it (or a `routeValidStrict` predicate).
5. **Dummy-hop padding is not modelled.** The model can append hops, but
   `BlindedHop` has no `isDummy` flag, so BOLT 4's `num_hops`-padding
   argument (`04-onion-routing.md:585-598`) is recorded in prose only.
6. **LDK's last-hop fee semantics differ.** LDK's `RouteHop.fee_msat` stores
   the final value (not a fee) on the last hop, while `Hop.feeMsat` is a fee
   on every hop. The anchors are therefore structural, not arithmetic
   equivalences; this is stated in the module docstring.
7. **Route search/optimality, liquidity, MPP, route hints, and
   `min_final_cltv_expiry_delta` policy are out of scope.**
