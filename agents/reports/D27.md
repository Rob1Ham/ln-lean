## Remaining gap / next step

The missing model field is the link between routing views/edges and the
announcement that authorises them. Wave A's `GossipNetwork` has independent
`gossip : GossipState` and `views : List ChannelView`, and `applyMessage`
does not create views from an accepted `channel_announcement`; it only
records the announcement. Closing `LN-RT-006` at the model level requires
extending `GossipNetwork` (or `ChannelView`) with a backing announcement
witness / direction-keyed announcement map and constructing views inside
`applyMessage` from `bindChannel`. Until then, `NetworkChannelsValid` must be
assumed, and the raw `applyGossip` path remains unauthenticated.
