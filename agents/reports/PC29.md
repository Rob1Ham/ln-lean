## Proof content and source references

* Amount.  `Invoice.amountSatisfies` is unfolded directly.  For a specified
  amount `a` the BOLT 4 window contributes `a.val ≤ paid.val`; the
  exact-payment side condition contributes `paid.val ≤ a.val`; antisymmetry
  on `Nat` then pins `paid = a` by cases on the `Msat` records.  The
  `none` branch is handled by the vacuous quantifier, matching the shipped
  definition.  Source theorem used conceptually:
  `LnLean.Proofs.Payment.InvoiceProps.amountSatisfies_some_iff`; the module
  does not restate or weaken it.
* Hash.  `Invoice.preimageCommits` is the relation `sha256 p = i.paymentHash`.
  The functional direction (`preimageCommits_determines_hash`) is
  unconditional.  The binding direction requires the explicit
  `HashInjective` (or `HashInjectiveOnValid`) hypothesis.  The bridge
  `paymentHashOf_injectiveOnValid` invokes the existing
  `LnLean.Crypto.paymentHash_commits`, which is the source theorem
  `LnLean.Crypto.Hash.paymentHash_commits`; the full form uses
  `PrimitiveModel.digest32_of_size` and `sha256_size`.  SHA-256 injectivity is
  never assumed here; the `CollisionResistant` /
  `PreimageResistant` fields of `PrimitiveModel` are passed as hypotheses.
* Expiry.  `Invoice.expiresAtWith i e = i.timestamp + e`; monotonicity is
  `Nat.add_le_add_left`, strict monotonicity is `Nat.add_lt_add_left`.  The
  valid-invoice corollaries carry `i.Valid` in the statement, though the
  arithmetic does not need it.  `not_expired_of_expiry_le` is the strict
  `would_expire` complement (BOLT 11 / LDK `:1573`).

   function, while `Crypto.Model.PrimitiveModel.paymentHashOf` uses the
   total `digest32` normalisation.  The bridge theorems close this seam

   injectivity is available or claimed.
