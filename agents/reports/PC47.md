## 2. Claim and composition

The module composes three existing lanes:

1. **Channel lock-in liveness** (`LnLean.Proofs.Liveness`):
   `Fair σ funding_locked` plus `NoLoss` takes an `awaitingLockin` channel to
   `normal`.
2. **HTLC liveness** (`LnLean.Proofs.Liveness`): a pending HTLC resolves when
   either a fulfil/fail settlement is eventually delivered or an
   `HonestTimer` reaches its `cltv_expiry`.
3. **Breach remedy timing** (`LnLean.Proofs.PhaseC3.WatchtowerBreach`): the
   explicit `DetectionBeforeDeadline` witness places detection at
   `detection.detectHeight` inside the half-open contest interval
   `[breachHeight, breachHeight + window)`; a `BridgeAcceptance` witness then
   makes the penalty valid, timely, and exclusive.

`WatchtowerLivenessScenario` carries **data only**.  The fairness, loss-free,
timer/settlement, watchtower-liveness, and L1-acceptance facts are theorem
hypotheses, so they can fail in the contrapositive.  Detection and the
penalty-transaction facts are fields of the scenario, exactly as in
`WatchtowerBreachRemedy`.

The model never assumes instantaneous detection.  `detection.detectHeight`
may be strictly greater than `c.breachHeight`; the only constraint is
`breachHeight ≤ detectHeight < breachHeight + window`, reused verbatim from
`WatchtowerBreach`.

## 5. What is deliberately not modelled

* No wall-clock timers: the schedule and `tip` are abstract, and `Fair` /
  `HonestTimer` are explicit hypotheses.
* No implication "live tower always reports": only the interface direction
  `reports → live` is used.
* No instant detection and no equality `detectHeight = breachHeight`.
* No Bitcoin script execution, signature checking, package relay, fee
  bumping, or HTLC-output penalties; those remain with `LnLean.Channel.Penalty`
  and `WatchtowerBreach`.
* The contest delay `window` is a parameter, not the old commitment's
  `to_self_delay` read from script.
