## 2. Claim and structure

The module defines a finite safety game with six actions
(`noop`, `networkDeliver`, `ledgerOp`, `l1Mint`, `l1Claim`, `l1Finalize`).
The adversary wins when it creates value (`CreatesValue`), owns the contested
output (`StealsContested`), or finalises two equivocating commitments
(`ConflictingFinals`: same signature/different body, or same payment
hash/different preimage).

The four assumptions are `Prop`-valued fields of `Assumptions`; none is a
global axiom:

| Assumption | Lean field | Failure predicate | Primitive / L1 interface |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signature unforgeability (C3) | `Assumptions.sigUnforgeable` | `SignatureFailure` | `GameParameters.verify` |
| Hash binding (C1) | `Assumptions.hashBinding` | `HashFailure` | `GameParameters.hash` |
| L1 timelock enforcement | `Assumptions.timelockEnforced` | `TimelockFailure` | `GameParameters.acceptsClaim` |
| Channel conservation | `Assumptions.channelConserved` | `ChannelConservationFailure` | `GameParameters.acceptsLedger` |

`AssumptionFailure P` is the required disjunction
`SignatureFailure P ∨ HashFailure P ∨ TimelockFailure P ∨
ChannelConservationFailure P`.

## 4. Remaining obligations

**None.** There are no `sorry`s and no missing fields needed by the stated
theorems.  The safe instance (`safeParameters`) and the genuinely winning
broken instance (`brokenParameters`, whose `acceptsLedger` is the constant
`true`) show that the reduction is non-vacuous in both directions.

The following are deliberate model boundaries, not Lean obligations:

* `GameParameters.verify` and `GameParameters.hash` are abstract interfaces.
  A concrete instantiation would use `PrimitiveModel.ecdsaVerify` and
  `PrimitiveModel.sha256`/`paymentHashOf`; the current statements take their
  algebraic consequences (`Crypto.EufCma`, `HashBinding`) explicitly.
* `GameParameters.acceptsClaim` and `GameParameters.acceptsLedger` are the L1
  bridge.  A concrete bridge would discharge `TimelockEnforced` from the
  BOLT 3/5 CLTV rule and `ChannelConserved` from
  `LnLean.Proofs.Conservation` (`CommitmentConserved` / ledger conservation).
* The game is deterministic (`strategy : Nat → GameState → Action`) and has
  no probability space; probabilistic polynomial-time security and
  negligible advantage are outside the model, as in `docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md`.
