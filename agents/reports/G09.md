### Statement fidelity
- **D17.** `waveD_d17_open_bounds` (D17 line 204) states, from
  `acceptOpenChannel p m = some a`:
  `m.dustLimitSatoshis < m.fundingSatoshis`,
  `minChannelValueSatoshis ≤ m.fundingSatoshis`, and
  `p.minFundingSatoshis ≤ m.fundingSatoshis`.  This matches the catalog
  wording "only above the dust and minimum-channel bounds" closely: strict
  dust, LDK's hard `MIN_CHANNEL_VALUE_SATOSHIS = 1000` (D17 line 90), and
  the acceptor's `min_funding_satoshis` policy.  D17 also proves the
  rejection directions and non-vacuity witnesses.  Its model is local:
  `OpenChannelMsg` contains only `fundingSatoshis`, `pushMsat`,
  `dustLimitSatoshis`, and `channelReserveSatoshis`; `accepts` (line 157)
  adds the BOLT/LDK checks.  There is no theorem connecting
  `acceptOpenChannel` to `HandshakeStep`, `Reachable`, `Channel.Step`, or
  `Channel.fundingValue`.
- **E17.** `waveE_e17_admissible_implies_bounds` (E17 line 189) states, from
  `Admissible policy r`:
  `(r.params.dustLimitSat : Int) ≤ r.funding.fundingSatoshis` and
  `(policy.minFundingSat : Int) ≤ r.funding.fundingSatoshis`.  This is
  non-strict on dust ("at or above", not "above"), and it uses only the
  policy minimum; it does **not** include LDK's hard 1000-satoshi bound.
  It is more model-grounded than D17: `OpenChannelRequest` composes the
  existing `FundingParams` and `ChannelParams`, and E17 contains a real

  a `negotiating` `Channel` with zero funding and a 1000-sat dust limit
  that still takes `Step.openChannel`.

  is declared in the exact catalog namespace and states
  `s.ready = true ↔ s.channelReadySent = true ∧

- D17 adds `OpenChannelMsg`, `AcceptPolicy`, `OpenChannelAcceptance`, and

## Reasoning
- The first two ranking criteria are ties: all four modules have zero real
  `sorry` sites, and all four exact-module artifacts are present, current
  relative to their sources, and consistent with the reported RC 0 builds.
- Both 17 main theorems are projection-like: D17's `accepts` and E17's
  `Admissible` contain the two funding bounds as conjuncts, so the positive
  direction is immediate once acceptance/admissibility is assumed.  Neither
  derives the bounds from independent transition semantics, and neither
  connects the acceptance predicate to `HandshakeStep` / `Reachable`.  This
  is a limitation of both lanes, not a discriminator between them.
- For target 17, D17 wins the statement-fidelity criterion.  The catalog
  claim says "only above the dust and minimum-channel bounds"; D17 proves
  the strict dust inequality, both the hard 1000-satoshi floor and the
  policy minimum, and the rejection directions.  E17 proves a non-strict
  dust inequality, omits the hard 1000-satoshi floor, and adds an
  unsupported `1 ≤ maxAcceptedHtlcs` requirement.  D17 also avoids E17's
  `MAX_MONEY` boundary weakening.  D17's local acceptor record is a model
  gap, but E17's `Admissible` is also a local predicate; neither closes
  `LnLean.Protocol.Handshake.open_bounds`.  On the plan's ordered criteria,
  D17's more complete statement outweighs E17's better model grounding.
- E17's counterexample is the strongest single piece of evidence in either
  17-module: it shows the actual `Step.openChannel` transition is
  amount-agnostic and therefore that the unconditional catalog claim is
  false of the current channel machine.  That is valuable, but it is a
  negative result about `Channel.Step`, not a proof of `open_bounds`, and
  under the stated ranking it does not overcome D17's statement advantage.
  If the parent weights "model grounding" above "bound completeness" for
  this target, E17 is the defensible override **only after** adding the
  hard 1000-satoshi conjunct, removing or justifying the
  `1 ≤ maxAcceptedHtlcs` clause, and adding the exact `open_bounds` alias;
  as written it is not a drop-in replacement.
- For target 18, D18 wins decisively.  It declares
  `LnLean.Protocol.Handshake.channel_ready_gate` in the exact catalog
  namespace, has no added hypothesis, and proves the trace-level "both
  events" property from `HandshakeState.initial`.  E18's
  `LnLean.Protocol.channel_ready_gate` is the wrong identifier for the
  catalog row and carries an unused `Reachable` binder.  E18's
  `ready_terminal`/`enter_ready_gate` facts are worth keeping as optional
  strengthening, but not at the cost of the exact target name.
- I did not choose on declaration count; the counts (D17 11, E17 13,
  D18 18, E18 16 named `wave*` declarations) are incidental.
