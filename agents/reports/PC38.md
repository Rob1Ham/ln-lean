## Declarations

`DetectionBeforeDeadline` and `BridgeAcceptance` are the two explicit
assumption structures.  `WatchtowerBreachRemedy` packages them with a revoked
commitment, a penalty transaction, a revocation witness, and the penalty
facts.  Every theorem below is fully proved; "conditional" means its
hypotheses are exactly the explicit fields of `WatchtowerBreachRemedy` (or its
stated arguments), not that a proof is missing.

## Remaining obligations / exact missing fields

No in-module `sorry` remains.  The assumptions that cannot be derived from
the chain model are represented as fields, and the report records them:

1. **Detection before the deadline** — field
   `WatchtowerBreachRemedy.detection : DetectionBeforeDeadline c window`
   (`detectHeight`, `breach_le_detect`, `detect_lt_deadline`).  Watchtower
   liveness does not imply this; a concrete liveness/monitoring policy is
   still external.
2. **Discovery route** — fields `WatchtowerBreachRemedy.online`,
   `WatchtowerBreachRemedy.tower`, and
   `WatchtowerBreachRemedy.detected : Detectable online tower c
   detection.detectHeight`.  `Watchtower.live` is only derived when the
   honest party is offline and a report exists.
3. **Bridge acceptance and confirmation time** — field
   `WatchtowerBreachRemedy.acceptance : BridgeAcceptance bridge p`
   (`confirmHeight`, `accepted : bridge.confirms p.toTx`), plus
   `detect_le_confirm` and `confirm_lt_deadline`.  Script execution,
   signature checking, fee bumping, and mempool inclusion remain behind the
   `L1ScriptBridge` interface.
4. **Penalty construction** — fields `penaltySpends`, `sweepValue`.  These
   are produced by the existing penalty layer; this module deliberately does
   not re-prove them.
5. **Contest delay semantics** — `window` is a parameter; equating it with
   the old commitment's `to_self_delay` is a script-semantics obligation.
6. **HTLC outputs, reorgs, fee economics** — out of scope, as documented in
   the module docstring.

Next smallest step: connect `window` to the `to_self_delay` stored in the old
`to_local` script, and connect `Watchtower.live` to a scheduling/monitoring
model.  Both require the script-semantics and liveness lanes and are therefore
not changed here.
