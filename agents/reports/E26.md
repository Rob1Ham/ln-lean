  - `waveE_e26_incomingExpiry_zero` — `incomingExpiry r f 0 = r.endExpiry f`.

## Remaining gap / next step

The arithmetic law itself is unconditional. What the model still does not
enforce is that each `Hop.cltvDelta` is the `cltv_expiry_delta` of a
*well-formed* `channel_update` for that direction: `routeValid` only checks
the edge's `short_channel_id`, enabled flag, and destination, not its
`cltvDelta` or timestamp. Likewise the receiver's
`min_final_cltv_expiry_delta` is not a model field, so `finalExpiry` is an
explicit parameter here. Those are separate model/obligation gaps; they do
not affect the accumulation theorem.
