## 4. What remains open and next smallest proof step

1. **`LN-CH-101` — full committed-update ledger.** `LnLean.Channel.State.Step`
   has no `update_add_htlc` constructor, so every "after any modelled update"
   theorem above is about a `Step` that leaves balances and HTLCs unchanged.
   The arithmetic core is already proved (`spendLocal_preserves_reserve`,
   `balanceDrop_preserves_reserveInvariant`). *Next smallest step:* add a
   single `balanceDrop`/HTLC constructor to the transition relation (owner of
   `Channel/State.lean`), then replace the hypothesis of
   `committed_update_preserves_ledger` with that constructor and discharge the
   ledger arithmetic over `htlcInFlightValue`.
2. **Net-of-second-stage-fee HTLC dust.** `dustTrimmedOutputs` uses the gross
   `amountSat` test, matching `Channel.Fee.isDust`, whereas BOLT 3 §2 trims on
   `amount - htlc_fee`. `gross_dust_rule_insufficient` is a checked
   counterexample (\(amount=1000\), \(fee=100\), \(dust=950\)): gross keeps
   the output, net trims it. *Next smallest step:* define
   `htlcNetDust dust fee h := isDust dust (h.amountSat - fee)` and split the
   HTLC branch of `dustTrimmedOutputs` so it uses that predicate; the
   `to_local`/`to_remote` branches already match BOLT 3.
3. **Two-sided reserve under a remote spend.** `spendRemote_preserves_remoteReserve`
   is proved, but no transition moves remote value; the channel-level
   combination is therefore not instantiated. This is subsumed by item 1.
4. **Reserve enforcement in signing.** `CommitmentValid` records the reserve
   condition as a predicate, but the wave-A model has no signing/acceptance
   relation that consumes it. The next step is to make the future
   `commitment_signed` guard require `CommitmentValid` (or at least
   `CommitmentReserveOk`).
5. **LDK `min` formula.** `maxHtlc_le_funding_of_ldk_bound` proves the
   arithmetic consequence once `maxInflight` is already bounded by the LDK
   remainder; it does not prove that `get_htlc_maximum_msat` returns exactly
   the `min`. That exact formula is a wire/handshake obligation, not needed
   for the capacity bound.

The gross-vs-net dust gap is recorded as a **counterexample**, not silently
weakened; the unconditional reserve/capacity claim is likewise recorded as
false and replaced by the handshake-conditional `ReserveWithinCapacity`.
