## Reasoning

The mechanical criteria tie: both candidates in each pair are `sorry`-free,
have unique (or at least unambiguous) obligation markers, and have exact-module
`.olean`/`.trace` artifacts newer than their sources. Neither selected file has
a real `sorry` site, and no report claims a green build that the artifacts
contradict. I did not run `lake build`.

For target 01, the decision is not close once the crypto parameter is checked.
E01's structural half is good, and its demo is a useful non-vacuity witness.
But every E01 theorem that mentions `PrimitiveModel` is vacuous in the current
model: `sha256_size` and `sha256_preimageResistant` are jointly inconsistent,
as proved by `Sha256Props.preimageResistant_vacuous`. D01 states the same
conditional bridge with a raw function and an explicit injectivity hypothesis,
so its conditional claims are meaningful, and it additionally proves
`p2wsh` injectivity, program uniqueness, explicit lexicographic order, and the
2-of-2 template directly. D01 is the better whole-file version. F01's
restricted 71-byte collision-resistance formulation and its
`WellFormed`-does-not-imply-the-link counterexample are the right hardening
ideas to port, but F01 is not a full target-01 artifact.

For target 02, both files are honest and both stop short of the catalog's
unconditional sentence. D02 is the safer whole-file choice because it stays
within the model's stated "only depth matters" abstraction, includes ledger
conservation, and ties the boundary to LDK's six-confirmation rule. E02 is a
credible alternate with a better-shaped independent `channelSafe` predicate,
but its reorg model is not pinned to the shared one: the rollback window is
`[fromHeight - depth, fromHeight - 1]`, leaving the block at `fromHeight`
untouched, and `waveE_e02_buried_anchor_preserved` sets `fromHeight` to the
observed `ChainView.height`. That is coherent only if `ChainView.height` is
the chain length; under the tip-height reading used by D02 and LDK it makes the
depth-1 counterexample rewrite the parent of the tip rather than the tip, and
makes the burial hypothesis one confirmation stricter than the standard
`confirmations > depth` rule (and one stricter than E02's own prose). Its
`unconditional_safety_false` is still a valid refutation *of its own chain
model*, but cannot be ported to the shared model without restating the window.
The choice is therefore medium-confidence, not a claim that E02 is wrong: if
the integrator prioritizes an independent safety predicate over an explicitly
pinned confirmation convention, E02 is defensible after documenting which
reading of `ChainView.height` it uses; if the goal is a whole-file
channel-safety artifact consistent with the existing model, D02 is the safer
keep.

Neither target should be marked "proved" in the catalog. `LN-L1-001` needs the
hash-link invariant (or a constructor that computes it); `LN-L1-003` needs
either D02's anti-reorg/settled assumption or E02's burial/finality premise.
The model gaps are real, not proof-script gaps.

## Overall recommendation

Keep **D01** for target 01 and **D02** for target 02. D01 is the clear choice
because E01's `PrimitiveModel`-based bridge is vacuous under the current
crypto interface; D02 is the safer whole-file choice because it preserves the
shared model's depth-only reorg abstraction, uses the standard
`depth < confirmations` arithmetic, includes ledger conservation, and matches
LDK's six-confirmation threshold (with the strictness caveat above). Treat E02
as a close alternate only after
pinning its `fromHeight`/`ChainView.height` convention; do not port its
concrete counterexamples as-is, and do not present either target as
unconditional.
