- Statement fidelity:
  - Proved unconditionally from the model (no added hypothesis) is the
    verification seam plus digest binding: acceptance under the counterparty
    funding key is exactly `ecdsaVerify` on `commitmentTag ++ sighash`, and
    C3 (`M.ecdsa_eufCma`) forces one signature to one digest. That is the

  - The literal "commitment signatures verify" (honest signature accepted) is

    verifier that ignores the key exists. No claim is made that a valid
    signature was *produced* by the key holder; secret-key ownership is not
    modelled.
  - The `commitment_signed` `htlcSignatures` list is out of scope; only the
    main signature is handled. The BOLT 3 sighash bytes, DER parsing, and key
    derivation are not modelled.

## Remaining gap / next step

Two model extensions would make the English sentence unconditional:
(1) add `ecdsaSign` plus a `SigningCompleteness` field to `PrimitiveModel`
(the honest-signature direction); (2) add a `KeyBinding` (or stronger
key-ownership) field to the ECDSA assumption suite, since `EufCma` alone does
not imply it, as `waveE_e06_eufCma_not_keyBinding` shows. A concrete BOLT 3
commitment sighash function, with a proof that it separates commitment numbers
and a bridge to `Channel.Commitment`, would discharge the
`Function.Injective sighash` hypothesis of the anti-replay theorem.
