## 4. What remains open / next smallest proof step

1. **Conservation is a hypothesis, not derived.** `Conserved` is a predicate
   over `Channel`; the module proves its consequences and shows that an
   accepted HTLC converts it to `out + in + pending + fee = funding`. It does
   not prove that the initial funding and every commitment construction
   satisfy it. Next smallest step: import `LnLean.Channel.Commitment` and
   prove that each `Commitment` satisfies
   `toLocalSat + toRemoteSat + Σ(htlc.value) + feeSat = fundingValueSat`,
   then relate that record to `Channel.balance`. Not done here because the
   brief limits imports to `Payment.MPP` and `Channel.State`.
2. **Reserve establishment is not proved.** `step_preserves_meetsReserve`
   covers `Channel.Step`, whose constructors never move balances. BOLT 2
   explicitly allows the reserve to be unmet initially
   (`02-peer-protocol.md:884`), so no unconditional theorem is claimed.
   Next step: model the initial-balance fields of `open_channel` /
   `accept_channel` and prove `MeetsReserve` under the negotiated initial
   balance premise.
3. **`availableOutbound` is not LDK's exact `outbound_capacity_msat`.**
   The module subtracts the reserve only; LDK also subtracts commitment fees,
   anchor values, dust exposure, and in-flight HTLC value
   (`channel.rs:6552-6605`). The simplified value is an upper bound. Next
   step: add the fee/anchor/dust deductions from `Channel.Fee` and
   `Channel.Commitment` and prove an equality with LDK's formula.
4. **MPP parts cannot be resolved to a channel.** `MPPPart.channel` is a
   `ShortChannelId`; `Channel` carries a `ChannelId` and no
   `short_channel_id`. `planFits` is therefore per-channel but
   channel-agnostic. Next step: add a `shortChannelId` (or resolution map) to
   the channel record, then prove that every part of a fitting plan names
   that channel.
5. **Other BOLT 2 checks are out of scope:** `max_accepted_htlcs`,
   `htlc_minimum_msat`, duplicate `id`, and CLTV expiry bounds. They need
   fields the wave-A `ChannelParams` does not carry.
6. **MPP atomicity/liveness** is not proved here; `mppComplete` and
   `mppAtomic` are used definitionally only through `mppComplete`.
