### Proof shape

`addParts ps` is `ps.foldl addPart []`.  The proof inducts on the `List.Perm`
derivation, generalising the accumulator.  The `cons` case is the induction
hypothesis at `addPart acc x`; the `swap` case reduces to the two helpers
`sumParts_addPart_comm` / `mem_addPart_comm`, then applies
`sumParts_foldl_addPart_congr` to the common tail.  `trans` composes.  The
final theorem instantiates the accumulator with `[]`.  All arithmetic is
`Nat` and closes by `omega`; no `Nat` subtraction is involved.
