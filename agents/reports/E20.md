    two commitments with different HTLC output lists of the same cardinality:
    the model pins at most the count, never the set.
  - The proved positive statements are exactly conditional on the coverage
    predicate: exact HTLC/signature counts, payload accounting, and fee
    uniqueness once the covered count, feerate, and anchor flag are fixed.

## Remaining gap / next step

The missing model pieces are:

1. a signed-commitment relation
   `CommitmentSigned → Channel.Commitment → Prop` (or a production function
   `Channel → CommitmentSigned` using the remote commitment), so that the
   `signature` field has an actual target;
2. a commitment number or explicit HTLC identifiers on `CommitmentSigned` if
   retransmission/duplicate detection is also to be covered; and
3. cryptographic signature verification (C3/C4), without which the
   signature bytes cannot certify the commitment contents or fee.

Once (1) exists, `waveE_e20_commitment_signed_covers` can be derived rather
than assumed, and the negative results above become statements about a
corrected model rather than the current one.
