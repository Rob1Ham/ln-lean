## 4. What remains open / next smallest step

No proof obligations remain in these five files. The honest boundaries are:

* `Step` models one event and the two commitment counters. It does not model
  the `AwaitingRemoteRevoke` queue (a second `commitment_signed` is held,
  not rejected), `channel_reestablish` retransmission, splicing,
  quiescence, dual funding, or `option_simple_close`.
* `commitmentOutputs` contains untrimmed HTLC outputs. BOLT 3's HTLC dust
  trimming (`dust_limit + HTLC fee`) belongs to the HTLC workstream; here
  the list is the post-trim list by construction.
* `Fee.lean` deliberately uses floor division (BOLT 3). `Basic.Core`'s
  `FeeRate.fee` uses ceiling division; the two are not identified, and
  commitment arithmetic always uses `feeForWeight`.
* Cross-module note for the integrator: `LnLean.Bitcoin.Seam` defines
  `commitmentBaseWeight = 724` with a docstring calling it the anchor
  variant. BOLT 3 says 724 is the **non-anchor** base and 1124 is the
  anchor base. `Commitment.commitmentBaseWeight` implements both correctly
  (724/1124); A1 may want to correct the Seam docstring.
* Next smallest step: connect `ProtocolEvent.updateFee` to
  `updateChannelFee` through `Step` (the effect is already present in the
  `Step.updateFee` constructor), then add a reachability lemma
  `normal` is reachable only through `fundingLocked`.
