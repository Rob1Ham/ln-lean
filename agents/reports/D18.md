## Remaining gap / next step

The literal BOLT rule also forbids `update_*`, `commitment_signed`, etc.
before both `channel_ready` messages.  `PeerState` has no such gate, so a
faithful peer-level theorem would need either a `ready` precondition in
`Peer.receive`/`Peer.send` or a separate `Operational` state field.  Until
then the achievable statement is the handshake-level gate proved here.
A two-endpoint model would also be needed to state the joint "both sides"
condition directly rather than as two local flags.
