# PC18 — `LnLean.Proofs.Channel.ReserveProps`

Target: `LnLean/Proofs/Channel/ReserveProps.lean` (repair, no new module).
Mission: remove the refuted unconditional committed-update ledger theorem,
keep its checked counterexample and the strongest true conditional form, and
leave the file with zero `sorry`.

## Result

The unconditional theorem `committed_update_preserves_ledger` was **deleted**.
It was false because `spendLocalBalance` uses truncated `Nat` subtraction: an
added HTLC larger than the settled local balance increases
`htlcInFlightValue` without decreasing `Balance.total`. Stale docstring text
naming the deleted declaration was rewritten to point at the counterexample
and the conditional theorem. No statement of any remaining theorem changed.

## Declarations (status)

| `committed_update_preserves_ledger_of_le` | `amount.val ≤ c.balance.localBalance.val` and the old settled+in-flight equation imply the successor's `total + htlcInFlightValue + fee = funding` after the modelled balance drop and HTLC append. | proved |
| `committed_update_preserves_ledger_counterexample` | there exist `c c' fee amount` satisfying the old equation and the update equation with `c'.total + htlcInFlightValue c' + fee ≠ c'.fundingValue`, i.e. the unconditional form is false. | proved |

Removed (not weakened, refuted): `committed_update_preserves_ledger`.
No remaining `sorry`, so no `OBLIGATION` marker remains in the file.

Supporting theorems used by the conditional proof:
`spendLocalBalance_total_val`, `inFlightSum_append_offered`,
`Balance.total_val`, and `omega`. The counterexample is discharged by
`simp`/`decide`-free reduction of the concrete zero-balance channel (no
`native_decide`).
