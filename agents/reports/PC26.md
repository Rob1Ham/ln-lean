Orientation is stated explicitly in the module docstring and by
`endExpiry_reverse_append` / `endExpiry_reverse_appendHop`: `Route.endExpiry`
is a left fold from `start` in hop-list order, whereas the BOLT 4 sender-side
expiry is read right-to-left from the recipient.  Because `Nat` addition is
commutative, reversal preserves `totalCltv` and the final expiry, while
reversing the order of accumulation.
