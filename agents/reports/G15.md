## Reasoning

**Target 29.** D29 is the clear choice. It is sorry-free, its exact-module artifact postdates its source, and it is the only file that declares the exact catalog name `LnLean.Payment.Invoice.payment_secret_match`. Its positive theorem is conditional on an explicit acceptance predicate, and its counterexample mechanizes why the amount window alone is insufficient. E29 is also sorry-free and has a good hash-plus-secret counterexample, but it omits the catalog alias and its report's claim that the target "cannot be stated" is contradicted by D29's compiled alias. E29's extra `checkPaymentSecret` and byte-level equivalences do not compensate for the missing catalog declaration. F13 is a different, model-hardening target with a real `sorry`.

**Target 30.** D30 is the stronger positive file: `collected_set_bound` covers any multiplicity-preserving sub-collection, `add_part_preserves_bound` is the literal "one more part" law, `add_part_within_remaining` states the actual remaining-budget guard, and `running_total_succ`/`running_total_complete` pin down the step and terminal behavior. E30 has the better counterexample and a useful `prefix_bound_of_total_le`, but its positive results are mostly a subset of D30's; it does not state the budget guard or the exact increment. Both are sorry-free and both have exact-module artifacts. D30's counterexample is mechanized but weak (an already-inconsistent one-part plan), and its "arbitrary arrival order" wording should be corrected; those are fixable defects, not reasons to prefer the weaker positive corpus. If the integrator weights counterexample fidelity above the extra positive lemmas, E30 is a defensible runner-up, but I recommend D30 as the whole-file base and porting E30's `overpayPlan` counterexample into it.

**F13/F14.** They are useful model-hardening references, especially F14's `runningTotalBound_add_iff`, but each has one actual `sorry`. Under the stated ranking (actual sorry sites first), they cannot displace the zero-sorry D/E files. They are not "missing-file" fallbacks because D29/E29/D30/E30 all exist.
