## 4. What remains open, and the next smallest step

1. **Model placement.** `XOnlyPubKey`, `tapTweak`, and `taprootOutputKey` are
   defined at the proof layer because wave A has no taproot module and the
   mission restricts imports to `Crypto.Sig`, `Crypto.Model`, `Basic.Core`.
   They should move to a wave-C model module (e.g. `LnLean/Crypto/Taproot.lean`
   or `Basic/Core`) so downstream proofs can share them.
2. **Key-type mismatch.** `PrimitiveModel.schnorrVerify` takes the model's
   33-byte `PubKey`, while BIP 340 uses 32-byte x-only keys. The bridge
   `XOnlyPubKey.toPubKey` prefixes `0x02` (the even-y BIP 340 lift). A faithful
   model would parameterise `schnorrVerify` over `XOnlyPubKey`; that is a
   `Crypto.Model` change and is out of B07 scope.
3. **Hash assumption strength.** Binding uses `PreimageResistant M.sha256`,
   i.e. injectivity on *all* byte strings. The narrower
   `CollisionResistant` field is restricted to 32-byte inputs and cannot be
   applied to the 64/96-byte tagged-hash preimages. This idealisation is
   stronger than real-world collision resistance and is unsatisfiable by a
   counting argument; it is the model's documented over-approximation
   (`docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md` C1), not a claim about SHA-256.
   *Next smallest step:* introduce a length-indexed
   `TaggedHashCollisionResistant` predicate (collision-freedom on inputs of a
   fixed length) and re-run the same four binding proofs with the weaker
   hypothesis; only the hypothesis type changes.
4. **Output key is a commitment, not a curve point.** `taprootOutputKey` is
   the 32-byte tweak digest. The real `Q = P + t*G`, its x-only encoding, and
   y-parity are not modelled; `taprootOutputKey_pair_injective` must not be
   read as a group-law theorem.
   *Next step:* add a tweak/group-operation interface (to `Bitcoin/Seam` or a
   secp256k1 model) with a `Q = addTweak P t` law and prove the x-only
   encoding is determined by `(P, t)`; this needs L1 group arithmetic that
   neither wave A nor the model currently exposes.
5. **BIP 341 sighash is abbreviated.** Only the `ext_flag` byte (`0x00` vs
   `0x01`) and an opaque tapleaf commitment are modelled. Prevouts, amounts,
   scriptPubKeys, annex, `spend_type`, `key_version`, `codesep_pos`, and input
   index are not. `tapSighash`/`keyPathDigest`/`scriptPathDigest` are therefore
   *not* the real BIP 341 digests.
   *Next step:* define a `SigMsg` record and prove the two digests are its
   key-path/script-path projections; the domain-separation proof then applies
   unchanged.
6. **Signature-length variant.** `SchnorrSig.Valid` fixes 64 bytes, so the
   65-byte `SIGHASH_ALL` form (`bolt-simple-taproot.md:205-207`) is provably
   absent. Supporting it requires a signature type carrying an optional
   sighash byte.
7. **Not modelled at all:** tapscript tree construction/branch hashing,
   control-block parsing and inclusion proofs, MuSig2/aggregation, signing and
   key generation, and any computational reduction. `schnorr_eufCma` is never
   proved; `acceptsAll_not_eufCma` shows the format laws alone do not imply it.
8. **Correction recorded.** The brief asks for "key-path and script-path
   verification use distinct tagged hashes". BIP 341 uses the single tag
   `"TapSighash"` for both; separation is the `ext_flag`/`spend_type` byte
   (plus tapleaf fields on the script path). The module states and proves the
   corrected claim: distinct *sighash domains* under one tag, with the
   `0x00`/`0x01` prefixes.
