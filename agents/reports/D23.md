## Remaining gap / next step

`GossipState` is append-only and keeps only a global `latestUpdateTimestamp`; it does not model per-`(short_channel_id, direction)` replacement. The per-channel rule is therefore proved against `GossipProps.ChannelView`, whose `applyUpdate` keys by `short_channel_id` but still does not decode the `direction` bit of `channel_flags`. A future model should add directional keys and per-direction clocks; the timestamp-necessity theorems here carry over unchanged. No mathematical gap remains for the stated timestamp claim under the current model.
