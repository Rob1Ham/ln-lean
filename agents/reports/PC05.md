Proof shape: `bytesToList (encodeTLV s)` is the concatenation of the record
encodings. Each record encoding is read by `parseRecord` using the wave-A
BigSize round-trip `decode_encode` for its type and length fields. The
fuel-bounded parser then consumes one record per fuel unit, and the encoded
stream size is at least the number of records. The `Canonical` hypothesis is
used through its `InDomain` component; sortedness/uniqueness are not needed
for this round trip, but remain essential for the uniqueness guarantee (the
parser itself accepts duplicates, see `decodeTLV_accepts_duplicate_types`).
