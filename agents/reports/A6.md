## 5. What remains open, and the next smallest step

1. **LN-RT-006 / gossip admission.** `GossipState.applyUpdate` and
   `applyGossip` do not require a successful signature check. The
   predicates and their conjunction-projection lemmas exist, but no
   admission invariant is stated. *Next smallest step:* parameterize
   `applyGossip` on a `SigVerifier` and prove that an accepted
   announcement satisfies `signaturesValid`; or add a `GossipState.WellFormed`
   predicate for announcements already in the store. This was left out
   deliberately because it changes the timestamp-theorem statements.
2. **`simplePaths` simplicity/completeness.** The definition avoids
   revisiting nodes via `contains`, and `simplePaths_head`/`_ne_nil` are
   proved, but no theorem proves `List.Nodup p` for every returned path,
   and no theorem proves completeness. *Next smallest step:* prove the
   DFS invariant "every node of a returned path lies in `visited` at
   return", which yields `Nodup` by induction on fuel.
3. **Route connectivity.** `routeValid` checks *per-hop* edge membership,
   not that consecutive hops share a node, and not capacity. This is
   called out in `Route.lean`. *Next smallest step:* add a `Route.Source`
   field (or an explicit source argument) and define a `Walk` predicate
   relating `hop_i.node` to the source of `hop_{i+1}`; prove that
   `constructRoute` of a path is connected.
4. **Fee search and rounding.** `Hop.ofEdge` rounds the proportional term
   up; BOLT 7 leaves rounding to the implementation, and `rust-lightning`'s
   exact arithmetic is not reproduced. There is no backwards fee search
   (`find_route`), no `min_final_cltv_expiry_delta`, no liquidity or
   capacity check. *Next smallest step:* define `findRoute` over a bounded
   `simplePaths` enumeration and prove that its total fee equals the sum
   of the chosen hops' fees (already the accumulation law).
5. **Gossip scope.** The timestamp clock is global, not keyed by
   `(short_channel_id, direction)`; BOLT 7's rule is per-direction. A
   global clock is conservative (it rejects some legitimate updates) and
   monotone, but not equivalent. Chain-hash filtering, UTXO checks,
   `gossip_queries`, and rate limits are out of scope.
6. **Onion cryptography.** `sealLayers` is a structural skeleton with
   empty ciphertexts/HMACs. There is no ChaCha20-Poly1305, HKDF, or ECDH;
   no privacy, unlinkability, or unforgeability claim is made.
   `OnionPacket.wireSize` is the fixed 1366-byte wire size, not a function
   of actual layer contents. `OnionError.packetSize_eq` is conditional on
   `data.size ≤ 256`; the wire encoder's truncation of larger data is not
   modelled.
7. **Anchors.** The reference clones are absent on this box (see §3), so
   `path:line` offsets were not machine-checked. A wave-B or integrator
   pass with the clones present should verify every offset.
