## Reasoning

**Target 43 — D43 over E43.** Both now build cleanly with no `sorry`, and both
state the same core fact. D43 is the stronger whole-file version for three
independent reasons. First, its central theorem is an unconditional equation
for the *entire* witness script, so the “revocation branch vs delayed-payment
branch” distinction is proved structurally, not inferred from fixed byte
offsets. Second, its revocation-key injectivity needs only 33-byte revocation
keys, and its delayed-key injectivity needs no key-size hypotheses at all;
E43's corresponding delayed-key and combined binding theorems require 33-byte
facts for all four keys. Third, D43 closes the loop to the abstract
`RevocationScheme`/`Shachain` derivation and names the two missing invariants,
which is directly relevant to `LN-L1-004` (“the to_local output carries the
revocation key”); E43 stops at the script/P2WSH level. E43's extra declarations
(17 vs 13) are mostly extraction/negation variants and do not outweigh those
differences. E43's explicit `branch_separator` is a useful audit artifact, but
D43's full decomposition already implies it.

**Target 44 — D44 over E44.** This is not close. D44 turns the BOLT 3 table
into a theorem about the model's actual transaction serializer: it builds a
`Tx` from `toLocalScriptPubKey` and `toRemoteP2WPKHScriptPubKey`, proves its
base serialization is 125 bytes, and derives the 724 weight with only the
witness term assumed. E44's numbers are correct, but they are definitions plus
`decide`; more importantly, its stated justification is factually wrong because
`serTx`/`serTxSize` exist in the vendored L1 model. Keeping E44 would regress
the target from a serializer-grounded proof to an arithmetic restatement.
E44's one salvageable idea is its finer witness split into header 2, witness
varints 5, and payload 217; that could be added as a follow-up lemma on top of
D44, not used to replace it.

No counterexample is needed for either target: the claims are true in the
current model. Neither lane supplies one, which does not affect the ranking.
