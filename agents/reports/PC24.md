# PC24 — per-commitment point/secret determines the commitment number

Target: `LnLean/Proofs/PhaseC2/RevocationNumber.lean` (new module).
Mission: under the shachain injectivity hypothesis already in the model,
prove that a published per-commitment secret/point identifies at most one
commitment number, and that `RevocationStore.isRevoked` is monotone in the
stored index.

Secret identification is *unconditional* relative to the existing model: the
injectivity is the field `Shachain.derive_injective`, surfaced by the existing
theorem `revocationSecret_injective`.  No collision-resistance assumption on
SHA-256 is introduced; the concrete shachain is outside the model.

Point and revocation-key identification are explicitly conditional on
`Function.Injective scheme.pointOf`.  This property is **not** present in the
`RevocationScheme` structure and is not implied by its `injective_point` field
(which concerns `derivePubkey` on `PerCommitmentPoint`).  It is the abstract
secp256k1 scalar-to-point injectivity assumption.

## Remaining obligations

None.  `grep -c '\bsorry\b'` is 0 and every theorem listed above is proved.
The remaining *model* gap is the missing point-injectivity interface: the
smallest next step is either adding
`pointOf_injective : ∀ {s t}, pointOf s = pointOf t → s = t` to
`RevocationScheme` (a model change outside this lane) or instantiating
`RevocationScheme` with a concrete secp256k1 group-law development.  A second
gap is that `RevocationStore.slots` is not related by any invariant to
`revocationSecret`; this module therefore reasons about `minSeen` only and
does not claim that a stored slot reconstructs a given secret.
