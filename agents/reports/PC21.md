## Module content

The module proves that `Commitment.totalOutputValue` is the BOLT 3 group sum
(`to_local + to_remote + HTLC outputs + anchors`), that it equals the sum of
the emitted `commitmentOutputs` list, and that under `CommitmentConserved` it
is `fundingValueSat - feeSat`. Anchor/no-anchor and zero-HTLC forms are
specialized; every conditional equality carries exactly its arithmetic
precondition.

Imports: `LnLean.Proofs.Conservation` and
`LnLean.Proofs.Channel.CommitmentTxProps` (narrower than `LnLean` and
sufficient; see build note below). `set_option autoImplicit false` is set.
