### Corrected / conditional forms

The brief asks for unconditional graph laws. Three of the requested claims
are false for the raw wave-A model, so the honest corrected forms are in the
tree and the gaps are recorded here:

1. **No self-loop.** `ChannelEdge` has no validity predicate, so a
   self-loop is representable. `model_allows_self_loop` proves this
   explicitly. The unconditional law is therefore stated for
   `Graph.WellFormed` (`findEdge_noSelfLoop`).
2. **Positive capacity.** `capacitySat : Nat` may be `0`.
   `findEdge_zeroCapacity` proves that `findEdge` can return such an edge.
   The positivity law is conditional on `Graph.WellFormed`
   (`findEdge_capacity_pos`). Fees and CLTV *are* unconditionally
   non-negative because they are `Nat` (`fee_params_nonneg`); that
   statement has no arithmetic content beyond `Nat.zero_le`.
3. **Symmetric `neighbors`.** `neighbors` is computed from directed edges,
   so symmetry needs the reverse direction to be present. The conditional
   law is `neighbors_symm`/`neighbors_comm` under `Graph.ReverseClosed`.
   `Graph.neighbors` also returns the source endpoint of a stored direction;
   hence `Graph.Walk` is the *adjacency* walk (either orientation), not a
   forward-directed walk. A forward-directed walk theorem would need
   `ReverseClosed` as an extra hypothesis.

## 4. What remains open and the next smallest step

1. **`LN-GRAPH-001` / `simplePaths_walk`.** The enumerator is assumed to
   produce walks; only `Nodup` and non-emptiness are proved. *Next smallest
   step:* extend the invariant in `simplePathsAux_nodup_aux` with a walk
   conjunct `g.Walk p` (with the walk rooted at `current`). In the
   `current = dst` branch it is trivial; in the recursive branch
   `next ∈ g.neighbors current` gives `g.Adjacent current next` by
   `adjacent_of_mem_neighbors`, the recursive result gives the tail walk,
   and `Graph.Walk` composes by `current :: q`. This is a local induction,
   not a definition change.
2. **Forward-directed paths.** If the route layer needs an edge in the
   queried direction, add `ReverseClosed` (or a direction-aware
   `neighbors`) and prove the forward version from `Graph.Walk`.
3. **Capacity is not a lookup precondition.** `findEdge` ignores capacity,
   HTLC min/max, and liquidity; route construction must check them. There is
   no `LN-GRAPH` obligation for this yet because `Route.lean` owns the
   capacity predicate; the counterexample `findEdge_zeroCapacity` records
   the graph-level gap.
4. **Well-formedness is assumed, not admitted by gossip.** `WellFormed` is
   a predicate; no theorem connects it to
   `LnLean.Routing.Gossip` signature/UTXO checks. That connection is the
   B22/wave-B gossip admission obligation (`LN-RT-006`).
5. **Finiteness is representational.** `edgeCount`/`nodeCount` are list
   lengths; `addEdge` may duplicate node entries and there is no
   `Nodup`/set semantics for `nodes`. No graph cardinality bound beyond the
   list representation is claimed.
6. **Not modelled at all:** fee computation for an amount, Dijkstra/A\*
   optimality, `simplePaths` completeness, diameter/connectivity, private
   channels, MPP, route blinding, and wire serialization.
