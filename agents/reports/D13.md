## Remaining gap / next step

- `LnLean.Channel.Channel` has no commitment-transaction fee/weight field,
  and `Step` has no fee precondition (its `commitmentSigned` constructor only
  checks `phase = normal`). The gate and `FeeCheckedStep` therefore carry the
  `Commitment` record as a separate index. The honest next step is a model
  extension putting the pending commitment (or at least its `feeSat` and
  weight) into `Channel`, then replacing `FeeCheckedStep` by a constrained
  `Step` constructor and deriving the gate precondition from the state.
- `update_fee` affordability against the channel reserve and dust exposure
  (BOLT 2 §Updating Fees) is LN-CH-006's subject and is not claimed here.
  The only interaction proved is rate monotonicity of insufficiency.
- BOLT 5 mempool/package-relay policy, RBF, anchor child-pays-for-parent,
  and `zero_fee_commitments` are outside the model; the gate is the BOLT 3
  base-fee check for the expected weight, not a relay simulation.
