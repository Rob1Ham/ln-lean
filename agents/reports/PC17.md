## Newly discovered gap (honest model note)

The refutation is due to `pushData` truncating the key length to `UInt8`:
`FundingParams` does not require 33-byte funding keys, so a key of length
≥ 256 makes the parser reinterpret key bytes (e.g. `0x51` as `OP_1`) and can
accept the whole script. `p.WellFormed` forces 33-byte keys and restores
rejection, but only because the wave-A `ScriptInstr` has no
`OP_CHECKMULTISIG` constructor
(`vendor/btc-lean-core/Bitcoin/Data/Script.lean:31-41`). A stronger future
model would either constrain the key type to 33 bytes at construction or add
a `checkMultisig` constructor plus real script semantics; neither is in scope
for this repair. Note also that even the conditional result says the wave-A
parser *rejects* the script, not that the funding output is spendable.
