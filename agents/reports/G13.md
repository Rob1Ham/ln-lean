The direction matters. `Route.endExpiry` is the model's forward fold (`Route.lean:148-150`), and `Hop.expiryAfter` documents BOLT's rule as “the incoming CLTV is the outgoing one plus `cltv_expiry_delta`” (`Route.lean:114-116`). E26's `incomingExpiry` is exactly the BOLT 4 incoming/outgoing relation: the expiry at hop `i` is the expiry at hop `i+1` plus hop `i`'s delta, and at `i = 0` it is the model's `endExpiry` from the recipient's `finalExpiry`. D26's `accumulated` is a cumulative forward sum over a prefix; adding `h`'s delta to the prefix value gives the next cumulative sum, but that prefix value is not the BOLT incoming expiry at `h` when `start` is the recipient's final expiry. D26's alias also lives in module `LnLean.Proofs.WaveD.D26`, not in the transcribed `LnLean.Routing.Route`, and its type mentions `LnLean.Proofs.WaveD.D26.accumulated`; it is therefore not a clean model-module declaration even though the name exists in the model namespace.

- E25 main theorems add only the explicit packet tuple and `I.verifyHmac tag l.hmac = false`; it has no semantic hypotheses. `checkedPeel` is a new definition, not a hypothesis. Its `waveE_e25_checkedPeel_never_final` also exposes that the checked path never produces a terminal payload; final delivery remains on `peel`.
- D26 main theorems add only the split witness `r.hops = p ++ h :: t`; its strictness result `waveD_d26_endExpiry_strict_of_pos` uses the weaker hypothesis `∃ h ∈ hops, 0 < h.cltvDelta`, which is a genuine improvement over the model's all-hops-positive `Route.endExpiry_gt'`. No semantic hypotheses.
- E26 main theorems add only the index bound `i < r.hops.length`; strictness corollaries add `0 < (r.hops[i]'hi).cltvDelta` or all-positive deltas. No semantic hypotheses.
- Shared model gaps: `OnionInterface.hmacBinding` is an uninterpreted `Prop` with no elimination principle and no authentic-HMAC predicate; `peel` is total and bypasses the check; there is no composite forwarding function that calls `openLayer`; `Route` has no absolute per-hop expiry function, `routeValid` does not require a hop's `cltvDelta` to equal its backing edge's delta (`Route.lean:278-280`), and there is no receiver `min_final_cltv_expiry_delta` field. Both 25 files therefore prove a statement about the checked operation, not about `peel`; both 26 files leave a model-level expiry-at-hop function to be adopted (E26 supplies one in its proof namespace and bridges it to `endExpiry` at index 0).

## Reasoning

For target 25 I prefer **D25**. It proves the catalog's soundness direction under the name `waveD_d25_hmac_sound`, the rejection and no-forward directions over the existing `openLayer`, and the sharp equivalence “a forward result exists iff the verifier accepts” (`waveD_d25_openLayer_forward_iff_accepted`). The counterexample is a single mechanized contrast between `peel` and `openLayer`, and the conditional C8-shaped theorem makes the cryptographic gap explicit without weakening the main results. E25 is also correct and builds, but its “not forwarded” theorem is about a newly introduced `checkedPeel` wrapper, so it proves the claim of a definition it added rather than of the model's existing `openLayer`; its `hmac_sound` name denotes the rejection direction and its actual soundness theorem is narrower. D25's `rejectingInterface` sets `hmacBinding := False` while E25's reject-all instance sets it to `True`; this is a cosmetic difference and is not used by any theorem.

For target 26 I prefer **E26**. Its `incomingExpiry` is the BOLT-faithful expiry-at-hop function: `incomingExpiry r f i = f + Σ_{j≥i} delta_j`, with the adjacent-step equality and at-least inequality, and with `incomingExpiry r f 0 = r.endExpiry f` and `incomingExpiry r f 0 = f + totalCltv` connecting it to the committed model. D26's `accumulated` is mathematically correct as a cumulative fold, and its `∃`-positive strictness is a nice bonus, but its prefix split is a forward-cumulative decomposition rather than BOLT's incoming = outgoing + delta relation; its route-level “at least” theorem compares against the final route expiry instead of the next hop; its `constructRoute` lemmas prove total and per-split contributions but not the pointwise `hops[i].cltvDelta = path[i].1.cltvDelta` (E26 proves that pointwise bridge); and its catalog alias mentions a proof-namespace definition and lives outside the transcribed model module. E26's only material defect is the missing `LnLean.Routing.Route.cltv_accumulates` declaration, which is an integration-time alias, not a proof gap.

F is not a candidate for either target: there is no F module for LN-RT-004/005, and the D25/D26 oleans under the F worktree are main's artifacts through the `.lake` symlink. D26 remains a defensible fallback if the release gate insists on the exact catalog identifier being present without adding an alias, but it should then be documented as the model's forward-fold reading rather than BOLT's adjacent-hop relation. I do not recommend importing both D26 and E26: that would leave two competing APIs for the same claim and would keep D26's proof-namespace `accumulated` in the model-namespace alias.
