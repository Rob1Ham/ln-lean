## 4. What remains open

1. **`LN-PAY-INVOICE-ENCODING`** — BOLT 11 bech32/charset encoding, HRP
   parsing, and the exact signable digest (HRP ‖ signatureless data part,
   SHA-256, 5-bit padding). `Invoice.verify` takes `digest` as a parameter
   for exactly this reason. This is deliberately not modelled.
2. **Signature cryptography** — `Invoice.verify` takes `sigCheck` as a
   parameter. A concrete instance must come from `LnLean.Crypto.Model`
   (C3 recoverable ECDSA), and no unforgeability is claimed here.
3. **`LN-PAY-MPP-OVERPAY`** — BOLT 4 allows the HTLC-set total to be
   `≥ total_msat` (and at most twice the invoice amount). `mppComplete`
   uses exact equality; the overpay variant and its safety proof are open.
4. **Payment-secret derivation** — LDK derives the secret by encrypting
   payment info and authenticating the hash. Only the 32-byte binding field
   is modelled; the derivation and its unforgeability are open.
5. **Preimage soundness** — `preimageCommits` is conditional on a
   caller-supplied SHA-256. Connecting it to `PaymentSession.settle`
   (settle succeeds only with a valid preimage) is a channel/crypto
   obligation, not proved here.
6. **Liveness** — `settle` is a pure transition relation, not a scheduler;
   eventual settlement under partial synchrony is separate.

**Next smallest step.** Add a `mppCompleteGe` predicate
(`target.val ≤ sumParts parts`) together with the theorem
`mppCompleteGe_no_part_exceeds_twice_target` under a per-part bound, so B26
(`LnLean/Payment/MPPProps.lean`) can close `LN-PAY-MPP-OVERPAY` without
touching these modules. Alternatively, B25 can import `Invoice.lean` and
prove that `Invoice.amountSatisfies` is downward/upward closed in the paid
amount.
