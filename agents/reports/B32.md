## 4. What remains open, and the next smallest step

Open (all recorded in the module docstring under "What is NOT modelled"):

1. **Transcription fidelity.** `theoremIndexIds`, `traceabilityIds`,
   `idTable`, `targetTable`, `provedTable`, `provedDeclarations`, and
   `lneanModules` are literal copies. The Lean theorems prove
   catalog ↔ copy agreement, not copy ↔ file-on-disk agreement. The scripted
   comparison above establishes the latter for this commit, but nothing
   regenerates it.
2. **Semantics of `proved`.** `proved_rows_have_decls` and
   `proved_decls_in_snapshot` are structural: they prove that a `proved` row
   names at least one declaration and that all named declarations are in the
   snapshot. They do **not** prove that the named declaration's type entails
   the English claim, and the snapshot membership itself is not a kernel
   environment query.
3. **Anchor resolution.** `no_unanchored_rows` proves only that no row uses
   the sentinel. That each `path:line` exists is an external check.
4. **`statementOnly` rows.** 35 of 48 rows remain unproved; this module makes
   no truth claim about them.
5. **Kernel string support.** Because `splitOn`/`startsWith`/`isEmpty` do not
   reduce in the kernel, the intended parse lives in table fields. A future
   toolchain with kernel-reducible string primitives would let
   `target_modules_lnean` and `id_areas_known` be stated over the raw
   `String` fields instead.

Next smallest step: wire a generator `scripts/gen-traceability.py` that
re-emits `idTable`, `targetTable`, `provedTable`, and the two doc id columns
from `LnLean/Proofs/Obligations.lean`, and make `scripts/check.py` fail on a
diff. That closes gap 1 mechanically. After that, the smallest semantic
step is a separate wave-B aggregation module that imports
`LnLean.Security.Invariants` and states, for one row (`LN-HT-001`),
`HtlcRecord.fulfill_sound` with its hypotheses instantiated from the catalog
record — the first genuine claim-to-declaration link.
