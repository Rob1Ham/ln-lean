## Reasoning

Both lanes produced sorry-free, exact-module-buildable Lean files, so the decision is about which whole file to integrate, not whether a proof exists. The ranking below uses actual `sorry` sites, artifact-level exact-module evidence, statement fidelity, strength/explicitness of added hypotheses, and counterexample quality; declaration count was not used.

For target 15, D15 wins on fidelity. It is the only file that (i) restricts both the offered/received/trimmed partition and the fee accounting to the live `htlcSet`, (ii) treats a trimmed HTLC's contribution as the gross floored amount that BOLT 3 adds to the fee, and (iii) starts from the model's `Balance` and an explicit conservation invariant rather than defining the second balance as a residual. E15's ledger is internally consistent, but its `toRemote` residual absorbs the second-stage fees, and its raw-list filters mean a caller can include settled HTLCs in a "live" commitment. The worked example makes the difference concrete: E15 adds 500 sat to the fee for a 600-sat trimmed HTLC with a 100-sat timeout fee; D15's `htlcSatSum (trimmedHtlcs ...)` adds 600 sat. E15's fewer hypotheses are therefore not a stronger result; they are bought by a definitional shortcut. D15's `hbal` is strong, but it is named, documented, and proved necessary by a checked counterexample, which is the right audit outcome for a model that has no balance/funding invariant.

For target 16, D16 wins on BOLT fidelity and on the quality of the negative result. The BOLT 2 rule is per sender ("for the first HTLC it offers: MUST set `id` to 0; MUST increase the value of `id` by 1 for each successive offer", `02-peer-protocol.md:2831-2833`), and rust-lightning keeps two independent counters. D16 formalizes exactly that with `HtlcBook`, `HtlcBook.Inv`, and `waveD_d16_push_preserves_inv`, and it proves that the global claim is false because both senders may legitimately use id 0 (`waveD_d16_bolt_allows_cross_direction_duplicate`). E16's global `IdsDistinct`/freshness guard is stronger than BOLT and would reject legitimate cross-direction reuse; its live-set variant is sound but does not supply the counter model. D16's counterexamples are therefore the more precise account of what the model does and does not guarantee.

The F-lane check does not change either recommendation. `F15.lean` and `F16.lean` are unrelated obligations (`LN-PM-KS` and `LN-SEC-ASYNC`), not alternative proofs of `LN-HT-003`/`LN-HT-004`; F15's `sorry` is outside this audit's target scope. There are no `Alt` files for targets 15/16.
