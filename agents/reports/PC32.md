## Remaining obligations / discovered gap

No proof obligation remains.  Model gap for the integrator: `Resolves` is
enabled for every pending HTLC regardless of any trigger
(`pending_htlc_resolves`), and `Channel.Step` never removes an HTLC from
`Channel.htlcs`.  Consequently the timer/delivery data is carried in the
`TriggeredResolution` witness rather than forcing the resolution step; the
`pending_htlc_eventually_timedout_...` theorem's `HonestTimer` hypothesis is
therefore a witness, not a proof of the transition's enabledness.  The
smallest next step is to add a trigger-indexed HTLC transition (or a joint
channel/HTLC step) that makes `Resolves` require the delivered settlement or
expired timer.
