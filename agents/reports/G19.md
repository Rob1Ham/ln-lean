### Statement fidelity

Target 37:

  Wave-C equation under `AffordableCommittedAdd`; the equation is stated with
  `ledgerValue c fee = local + remote + pending + fee`, and

  `Balance.total + htlcInFlightValue + fee`.

## Reasoning

**Target 37 — E37 over D37/D37Alt/F03/F11.**
1. *Sorry sites:* E37 has 0; D37 (current) has 1 on a statement it refutes in
   the same file; D37Alt had the same sorry and is now deleted; F03 has 1;
   F11 has 0 but no ledger theorem. E37 is the only version that both proves
   the catalog ledger claim conditionally and eliminates the false
   unconditional statement by proving its negation.
2. *Build:* E37's exact-module artifact postdates its last source edit
   (00:16:02 → 00:17:59) and contains no `sorryAx`. D37's artifact is also
   green, but it contains `sorryAx`, i.e. a green build does not mean a
   sound development here.
3. *Statement strength:* E37 proves the sharp `iff` for the ledger
   (`waveE_e37_ledger_iff_covered`), a delta form that does not assume the old
   ledger equation (`waveE_e37_covered_preserves_ledger_delta`), preservation
   of the Wave-C `ReserveInvariant` and the receiver's in-flight cap
   (`waveE_e37_affordable_preserves_accounting`), and the agreement with
   `ReserveProps.committed_update_preserves_ledger_of_le`.
4. *Non-vacuity / counterexample:* E37 has an explicit affordable witness
   (`waveE_e37_affordable_witness`) and a non-degenerate failure witness
   (`local = 0`, `remote = funding = 1000` msat) plus the universal negation
   `waveE_e37_unguarded_claim_false`. D37's counterexample uses the
   degenerate zero-balance/zero-funding channel and it has no affordable
   witness.
5. *D37's genuine advantage* is the guard characterization
   `waveD_d37_affordable_iff_remaining` and the reserve-only guard. That is
   useful, but it does not outweigh a `sorry` on a false theorem and the
   missing sharp ledger result. If D37 were later made sorry-free and kept its
   guard characterisation, it would be a credible second; at this snapshot it
   is not keepable.
6. `F03` is a broader arithmetic module but uses the weaker fits guard, has a
   `sorry` at `LN-WF-F03`, and is not the target module. `F11` is a
   reserve/exposure module with no ledger conservation, so it cannot close
   `LN-CH-101`.

**Target 38 — D38, narrowly over E38.**
Both are sorry-free, green, and equally faithful on the main conditional
statement. The tie-breakers are:
1. `D38`'s counterexample theorem states the strong fact that the channel is
   *already announced* as part of the existential, so the theorem itself shows
   the gap is key attribution rather than a missing announcement. `E38`'s
   witness has the same shape but the announcement-existence fact is only in
   the private witness construction, not the theorem statement.
2. `D38` includes `waveD_d38_announced_key_admission_accepts` and
   `..._attributed`, mechanised positive witnesses that the gate accepts and
   attributes a real update. `E38` has no non-vacuity witness for its gate.
3. `D38`'s generic `applyUpdateIfAdmitted` is a clean reusable admission seam;
   `E38`'s extra conditional against Wave-A
   `acceptChannelUpdate` (`waveE_e38_acceptChannelUpdate_signing_key_announced_of_admission`)
   is nearly circular: its hypothesis is
   `u.signatureValid verify → announcedKey ...`, which already supplies the
   conclusion once signature validity is known. This is documented as an
   isolation lemma rather than an independent result.
`E38`'s real extras are `verifiedAnnouncedKey` (announcement signature
authenticity) and monotonicity under arbitrary `GossipMessage`
(`waveE_e38_announcedKey_applyGossip`). They are worth folding into a future
replacement, but they do not make E38 the better whole-file keep at this
snapshot.
