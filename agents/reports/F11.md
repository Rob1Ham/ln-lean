# F11 — HTLC exposure and reserve bounds

## Target
- Marker: `LN-WF-F11`
- Module: `LnLean.Proofs.ModelHardening.F11`
- Parent obligation: `LN-CH-RESERVE`

## Result
- Status: proved
- Build: RC 0 (the only build warning is the pre-existing
  `ReserveProps.committed_update_preserves_ledger` `sorry`; this module has
  no `sorry`)
- Mechanised negative result:
  `waveF_f11_inadmissible_update_breaks_reserve`
- New definitions:
  - `waveF_f11_Update` — proof-level `update_add_htlc` record
    (`direction`, `amount`, `paymentHash`, `cltvExpiry`).
  - `waveF_f11_stateFor`, `waveF_f11_htlcOf`, `waveF_f11_spendFor`,
    `waveF_f11_apply` — the update effect (balance drop plus appended `HTLC`).
  - `waveF_f11_inFlightFor`, `waveF_f11_capFor`, `waveF_f11_minFor` —
    per-direction HTLC exposure, the receiving endpoint's
    `max_htlc_value_in_flight_msat`, and its `htlc_minimum_msat`.
  - `waveF_f11_ExposureInvariant` — both per-direction exposure bounds.
  - `waveF_f11_Admissible` — the reusable admissibility predicate: normal
    phase, positive amount, expiry bound, minimum, exposure cap, and reserve
    guard.
- Main theorems:
  - `waveF_f11_local_spend_reserve_iff` /
    `waveF_f11_remote_spend_reserve_iff`: under the non-truncation premise
    `amount <= balance`, the post-spend reserve check is equivalent to
    `reserve * 1000 + amount <= balance`.
  - `waveF_f11_apply_local_reserve_iff` /
    `waveF_f11_apply_remote_reserve_iff`: the post-update reserve conjunct is
    exactly the corresponding guard in `waveF_f11_Admissible`.
  - `waveF_f11_admissible_preserves_reserveInvariant`: an admissible update
    preserves `ReserveInvariant`, for both offer directions.
  - `waveF_f11_inFlightFor_apply`, `waveF_f11_inFlightFor_apply_of_ne`,
    `waveF_f11_capFor_apply`, and
    `waveF_f11_admissible_preserves_exposureInvariant`: adding an HTLC adds
    exactly its amount to its direction's in-flight value; an admissible
    update preserves both exposure bounds.
  - `waveF_f11_inadmissible_update_breaks_reserve`: a mechanised update with
    local balance 2000 sat, local reserve 1000 sat, and a 1500 sat offered
    HTLC.  The statement explicitly gives the five passing admissibility
    conjuncts, proves `¬ waveF_f11_Admissible`, and proves the successor
    violates `ReserveInvariant`.
- Added hypotheses:
  - The preservation theorem assumes the old `ReserveInvariant` and
    `waveF_f11_Admissible`; these are the intended preconditions, not
    weakenings.
  - The exact iff lemmas assume `amount <= balance` (no truncated
    subtraction).  This is implied by the reserve guard itself
    (`reserve * 1000 + amount <= balance`), so the main theorem does not need
    it as an extra premise.
  - The direction-indexed apply lemmas assume the update direction
    (`.local` or `.remote`); both cases are covered by the preservation
    theorem.
- Statement fidelity:
  - "Admissible updates preserve the reserve invariant" is proved
    unconditionally relative to those two predicates, for both directions.
    There is no `sorry`, `axiom`, `admit`, `native_decide`, or `unsafe`.
  - The exposure cap is direction-faithful: an HTLC we offer is bounded by
    the remote endpoint's advertised `max_htlc_value_in_flight_msat`; an HTLC
    the peer offers is bounded by ours.
  - The reserve arithmetic is exact, not merely sufficient: the guard is also
    necessary for the reserve conjunct under the non-truncation premise.
  - The negative result is mechanised separately so the unconditional
    "every update preserves the reserve" claim is not asserted.

## Missing model field / next step
- `LnLean.Channel.State.Step` has no `update_add_htlc` constructor and never
  changes balances or `htlcs`; `waveF_f11_apply` is therefore a proof-level
  operation, matching the `ReserveProps` treatment of `spendLocalBalance`.
  The model-level bridge is still obligation `LN-CH-101`.
- There is no channel-capacity field in `ChannelParams`;
  `ReserveWithinCapacity` in `ReserveProps` is a predicate.  Our predicate
  uses the reserve guard directly, so it does not need capacity.
- `LnLean.Channel.HTLC.addHTLC` operates on `HTLCRecord`, while
  `Channel.htlcs` is `List HTLC` (Types).  There is no committed bridge
  between these records; `waveF_f11_htlcOf` constructs the `HTLC` directly.
- Not modelled here: the fee estimator, commitment-level HTLC exposure, the
  483 accepted-HTLC cap, and the `option_anchors` weight effects.
