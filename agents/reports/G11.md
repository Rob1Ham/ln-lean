## Reasoning

For target 21, the two files are both `sorry`-free, both have exact-module artifacts, and both prove materially the same positive core: sending a non-handshake channel message records it; receiving a `channel_reestablish` leaves the addressed record and its retained copies unchanged; and `resend` returns the retained message. The choice turns on disclosure and coverage. E21 is shorter and has the cleaner locator lemma, but it overstates its result: its `retransmittable` predicate includes `funding_signed` and `channel_ready`, yet its `waveE_e21_send_then_resend` hypothesis `sendEvent? m = none` excludes exactly those two kinds, and the report labels the file "proved" without mentioning the gap. D21 proves the same positive result, proves the no-rollback law for every non-handshake receive (not just `channel_reestablish`), and adds a precise negative theorem showing that a legal `funding_signed` send leaves nothing for `resend`. The extra `hfc'` hypotheses in D21 are technical post-state facts that follow from the model; they do not weaken the semantic claim. Choosing E21 would hide a real model defect; D21 exposes and localizes it. D21 is therefore the better whole-file version, but LN-PR-005 should be recorded as conditional/partially blocked, not as proved, because two of the five BOLT 2 kinds cannot be retransmitted by this model.

For target 22, both files are `sorry`-free, built, and correctly refuse to claim the unconditional result: BOLT 1's `negotiated` rule is asymmetric (`lcl.requires n || mutualSupport`), so it equals the support intersection only on a surviving connection. E22 supplies the more complete and more faithful characterization of the catalog claim: an unconditional "intersection implies negotiated" direction, the pointwise equality under `hAccept`, a literal set equality between the negotiated set and the intersection of the two support sets, a symmetric-negotiation theorem, and a BOLT 2 channel-type subset law. D22's bitwise soundness and completeness condition are useful, but E22 already proves the required negative bitwise result and covers the main claim more directly. D22 also has two quality problems: its `waveD_d22_channel_type_anchors_imply_static` applies `ChannelTypeAnchorsImplyStatic` — which `FeaturesProps.lean` documents as a channel-type predicate "deliberately not asserted for a bare `init` vector" — directly to the peers' feature vectors, and the docstring of `waveD_d22_bitwise_intersection_not_support_intersection` describes feature 6/bit 12 but the theorem uses the feature-0 witnesses `evenOnlyZero`/`oddOnlyZero`. Neither is a soundness bug, but both reduce fidelity. E22 is the better whole-file version for target 22; the catalog row must remain conditional on `hAccept`.
