# F16 — Async safety / fairness separation

## Target
- Marker: `LN-WF-F16`
- Module: `LnLean.Proofs.ModelHardening.F16`
- Parent obligation: `LN-SEC-ASYNC`

## Result
- Status: conditional (safety proved unconditionally; liveness conditional, with a mechanised drop-all counterexample)
- Build: RC 0
- New definitions:
  - `AsyncSystem State Label` with `start`, partial `step?`, `run`, `Eventually`, `FairlyScheduled`.
  - `AsyncInvariant sys` with `pred`, `init`, `preserved`: the reusable asynchronous transition invariant.
  - `ledgerSystem`, `ledgerInvariant` (instantiation on `LnLean.Security.Ledger`).
  - `commitmentSystem`, `commitmentInvariant` (instantiation on `LnLean.Proofs.CommitmentConserved`).
  - `dropAllSchedule`, `dropAllAdversary`, `ledgerGame` (the model's `SecurityGame` vocabulary).
  - `SettlementState`, `SettlementLabel`, `settlementStart`, `settlementStep?`, `settlementSystem`, `settlementInvariant`, `schedulePolicy` (one-message HTLC settlement instance).
- Main theorems:
  - `AsyncInvariant.holds` and `AsyncInvariant.safety_without_fairness`: every successful prefix of every schedule satisfies the invariant, by induction. No fairness hypothesis occurs in the type.
  - `ledger_safety_without_fairness`: every prefix of any `LedgerOp` schedule conserves `funding = local + remote + in-flight + fee`.
  - `commitment_safety_without_fairness`: every prefix of any schedule of conserving commitment steps keeps `CommitmentConserved`.
  - `ledgerGame_secure`: `Secure (ledgerGame l0 σ)` for every operation schedule `σ`; the bad predicate (non-conservation) is unreachable at every finite horizon without fairness.
  - `AsyncInvariant.eventually_of_fair`: liveness from an invariant plus an explicit fairness hypothesis.
  - `settlement_safety_without_fairness`: the pending HTLC is never lost under a dropped message.
  - `settlement_live_of_fair`: if `deliver` is fairly scheduled at a live prefix, some finite prefix settles the HTLC.
  - `dropAll_never_settles`, `dropAllAdversary_never_settles`: the drop-all schedule never reaches `settled = true`.
  - `liveness_requires_fairness`: the unconditional statement "every schedule eventually settles" is false.
  - `safety_fairness_separation`: conjunction of unconditional safety, conditional liveness, and the drop-all failure.
- Added hypotheses:
  - Safety: only initial conservation `h0 : l0.Conserves` and one-step preservation (supplied by `Ledger.step_conserves` or by the caller for commitment steps).
  - Liveness: `AsyncSystem.FairlyScheduled sys σ l`, i.e. the progress label is selected at a prefix index whose execution is live. This is a parameter, never an axiom.
- Statement fidelity:
  - The safety statement is the faithful unconditional async claim: `∀ σ n s, run σ n = some s → Inv s`. It quantifies over all schedules, including unfair and stuttering ones. `step? = none` models a dropped/illegal label and is not treated as progress.
  - The liveness statement is stated conditionally and is not weakened: `FairlyScheduled` is exactly the missing fairness premise. Its necessity is proved negatively by the tick-only/drop-all schedule, not assumed.
  - No `sorry`, `axiom`, `admit`, `native_decide`, `unsafe`, `@[implemented_by]`, Mathlib, or Wave-D/Wave-E import. The single `-- OBLIGATION: LN-WF-F16` marker is adjacent to the liveness section.

## Missing model field / next step
- `LnLean.Security.Model` has no labelled transition relation or enabledness predicate. `SecurityGame.step` is a total function, so a dropped message and a protocol stutter are indistinguishable. `AsyncSystem.step?` is the missing partial layer; a future model revision should expose `step? : State → Label → Option State` and derive `SecurityGame.step` by totalising `none` as a stutter (as `ledgerGame` does here).
- `NetworkSchedule` records delivery capability but is not connected to protocol transitions. `schedulePolicy` connects one message and node to a settlement label; a general bridge `NetworkSchedule → (Nat → Label)` (or an enabledness predicate per message) is the next integration step.
- `SecurityGame` has no fairness field. Liveness obligations need a fairness predicate in the game record (or a separate `Fair` hypothesis on the schedule). The `FairlyScheduled` definition here is a small, reusable candidate.
- The settlement instance abstracts one in-flight HTLC to two booleans; a follow-up should connect it to `LnLean.Channel.HTLC` / `LnLean.Security.HtlcRecord` and to retransmission (`channel_reestablish`) so that a dropped `update_fulfill_htlc` is the concrete model of `tick`.
