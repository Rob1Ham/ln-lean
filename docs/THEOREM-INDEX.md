### Proof-wave promotion notes

The following rows were promoted from `statement-only` after exact, sorry-free
theorems were added. The theorem names resolve, but the scope caveats are part
of the row's meaning and are also recorded in the lane reports under
`agents/reports/`.

* `LN-RT-004` (`Onion.hmac_sound`) states the checked `openLayer` rejection
  property. Raw `peel` is the structural peel and intentionally bypasses HMAC;
  the mechanized counterexample in `LnLean.Proofs.WaveD.D25` is preserved.
* `LN-PM-002` (`Invoice.signature_valid`) is the model-level projection from
  `Invoice.verify`; it does not prove ECDSA soundness. ECDSA unforgeability and
  key binding remain assumption C3.
* `LN-SEC-002` (`Conservation.global_conservation`) covers the additive
  multi-channel ledger only. `MultiChannelLedger` has no L1 spend-set
  component; the L1 ledger is modelled separately (`LnLean.Security.L1Ledger`
  and `LnLean.Proofs.WaveD.D32`), so the theorem does not assert the L1 half of
  the English sentence.
* `LN-SEC-003` (`Security.safety_async`) quantifies over arbitrary finite
  adversarial action lists with no fairness hypothesis, but keeps the four
  explicit `Assumptions P` fields (signature unforgeability, hash binding,
  timelock enforcement, channel conservation) as hypotheses.
* `LN-SEC-006` (`Liveness.watchtower_bridge`) requires
  `wt.reports c c.breachHeight`, which implies `wt.live`. A live tower alone
  is insufficient; `LnLean.Proofs.WaveE.E35` contains the counterexample.

The proof-wave 2 promotions remain valid and keep their scope notes:

* `LN-CR-001/002/003` are standalone conditional contracts. `paymentHash_sha256_contract` covers constructed/opened hashes and C1 preimage uniqueness; D05's well-formed 33-byte counterexample refutes the universal range reading. `commitmentSig_contract` covers C3 message binding and funding/close domain separation; it does not claim signing completeness or key attribution. `onionHmacTag_size_and_bindsOn` covers 32-byte tags and finite-execution C8 binding; global HMAC PRF is refuted by D07.
* `LN-CH-002` proves nonnegativity unconditionally and the funding bound under the BOLT 3 balance equation; D46 retains the unconditional counterexample.
* `LN-HT-003` is a partition theorem under the explicit ledger invariant.
* `LN-PR-001` is the D17 acceptor-bound theorem. `LN-PR-006` is the support-set intersection under BOLT 1's survival hypothesis, not raw bitwise AND.
* `LN-SEC-005` is the bounded-schedule theorem (`FeeScheduleBounded`); D34 retains the unconditional fee-spike counterexample.

### Proof-wave 3 promotion notes

Proof-wave 3 retargeted and promoted the last eleven rows. Each row now names an exact, sorry-free theorem; the following caveats are part of the row's meaning:

* `LN-L1-001` now combines the byte-template theorem with `FundingLinkTargets.fundingScriptPubKey_eq_p2wsh_ordered` (the handshake constructs the hash link) and `InterpreterBridgeTargets.funding_interpreters_agree` (the vendored P2WSH verifier agrees with the model under explicit signature/context laws). C1 still covers 32-byte payment preimages, not arbitrary 71-byte witness scripts; this is a positive-direction bridge, not unconditional consensus spendability.
* `LN-L1-003` (`reorg_preserves_channel_safety` plus `deep_reorg_separate_failure_mode`) keeps a common chain tip, common-prefix confirmation arithmetic, and the bounded-depth invariant explicit. `safety_not_unconditional` records why the unconditional reading is false.
* `LN-L1-004` now targets the full two-branch BOLT 3 `toLocalScriptFull` model plus `InterpreterBridgeTargets.toLocal_delayed_interpreters_agree`. Containment is unconditional; commitment assumes 33-byte keys and `to_self_delay < 2^16`, and the output-level form assumes an injective `sha256` plus `HashesMatch`. The delayed branch executes through the vendored `OP_CSV`/`OP_CHECKSIG` path under explicit decoded-delay and sequence laws; the revocation branch remains model-only, and the legacy flat `toLocalScript` is retained.
* `LN-L1-006` (`commitmentBaseWeight_model_target`) covers the BOLT 3 fee-basis constants `724`/`1124` and `172` per untrimmed HTLC. The anchor constant is a negotiated fee basis, not a literal serialization weight: `weightTargets_serializedWeight_anchor_gap` proves the exact 8 WU gap. Witness serialization and the CompactSize threshold remain explicit gaps.
* `LN-CR-005` (`wellFormed_iff_pairwise` plus `wellFormed_record_type_unique`) is the exact theorem-level characterization of TLV well-formedness. It includes an exact admission checker and an `InDomain` round trip; the out-of-domain counterexample is retained.
* `LN-CH-003` combines `output_ordering` with the net/gross layer: `migratedFrom_outputsNet_perm_commitmentOutputs` proves the canonical legacy permutation at arbitrary dust from `CommitmentTx`, and the effective-fee accounting is explicit. D12 still refutes the unconditional legacy `commitmentOutputs` reading; the legacy function is unchanged and the literal `Types.lean` fields remain an integration patch.
* `LN-CH-005` (`commitmentSigningGate_rejected_iff_insufficient` plus `..._iff_no_checkedSigning`) is the exact fee-gate characterization, with the normal-phase transition form explicit.
* `LN-HT-004` is per-sender uniqueness (`dir_id_unique`), the explicit `CrossDisjoint` untagged global form, and a direction-tagged namespace (`tagged_id_unique_of_reachable`, `tagged_ids_nodup_of_reachable`). BOLT 2 ids are per-sender and both counters start at zero; `reachable_cross_direction_duplicate` still mechanizes the failure of the literal untagged global claim, while `cross_direction_duplicate_tagged` shows the tagged ids are distinct.
* `LN-PR-003` keeps `funding_signed_signing_contract` and adds `SighashTargets.funding_and_commitment_signing_contract`, a joint funding/commitment contract under an explicit `FundingSigningLaw` and `KeyBinding`. C3 alone gives neither signing completeness nor key attribution; the lane includes a counterexample and no secret-key model.
* `LN-PR-004` now combines the `CommitmentSignedFull` coverage theorem with `SighashTargets.SighashProduces.coverage`. A BOLT 3-shaped structural serializer derives the commitment digest/signature and per-HTLC digests/signatures under C3; the encoding is not bit-exact, C3 binds signatures to digests rather than views, and `ProducedFrom` alone still does not determine the digest. The legacy wire record alone determines neither the HTLC set nor the fee; the negative results are retained.
* `LN-RT-006` (`gossip_graph_only_valid_announcements`) covers reachable states built by the signature-gated `admitGossip` transition. Raw `applyGossip` remains unfiltered; the verifier is abstract and G19 key attribution is separate.

No row is silently weakened: every caveat above is also recorded in the row's source comment and under `agents/reports/swarm3/`. Remaining full-verification gaps are tracked in `docs/OPEN-ISSUES.md`.

### Proof-wave 4 bridge and conformance notes

Proof-wave 4 did not add catalog rows. It added six modules and strengthened two
targets; the following are the significant caveats:

* `SpendTargets.lean` defines an explicit stack-machine semantics and proves the
  serializer equal to the seam byte templates. Funding 2-of-2 and both
  `to_local` branches are conditional on `InterpreterLaws`; sighash is a
  parameter, CHECKMULTISIG is 2-of-2, NULLDUMMY is ignored, and CSV/CLTV are
  abstract. It is not a Bitcoin consensus interpreter. A real vendored
  interpreter exists but is not imported (`L1-07`).
* `CommitmentIntegrationTargets.lean` computes second-stage fees from the
  feerate, proves the exact dust threshold and trimming partition, and proves
  `legacyOutputs` is a permutation of the rich legacy view under zero dust.
  The rich model still lacks the gross/net fields needed for BOLT-3-exact
  values (`CH-03`).
* `HtlcGlobalIdTargets.lean` proves global uniqueness for the direction-tagged
  `HTLCTag` namespace. The wire remains per-sender; the tag is a model-side
  namespace, not a new BOLT 2 encoding.
* `ProtocolWireTargets.lean` proves conditional exact coverage from
  `ProducedFrom`. Signatures/digests are supplied production data, not derived
  from a sighash serializer; `PR-02` is mitigated, not closed.
* `GossipAttributionTargets.lean` proves `admitGossipAttributed` edges have a
  signed update and an announcement key. Endpoint attribution needs explicit
  `AttributionConsistent`/`KeyAttribution`; raw `applyGossip` is unchanged.
* `ConformanceTargets.lean` proves 86 vector expectations for BigSize/TLV. The
  suite is vector conformance, not a differential implementation test; two TLV
  value/unknown-type cases are declared model gaps.
